# Heretics and Tribrids

Both are blends of the species on the other pages.

## Heretics

A **heretic** is a **siphoner who joins the vampires** (R63).

- In the **vampire faction**.
- **Keeps siphoning** (see [Witches and siphoners](Witches-And-Siphoners#siphoner)).
- **Keeps the witch bonus**: altar range x1.5 and rite cost x0.75.
- **Prey** for hunters (a vampire type).
- A heretic cannot become a hybrid: the gene kill and the Rite of Lineage (Awaken) are refused ("A siphoner cannot become a hybrid.").
- Compulsion works for a heretic from Vampirism level 10, like any vampire (see [Sirelines](Sirelines#compulsion)).
- Tensura (Tensura part): skills `drain`, `charm`, `self_regeneration`, +20% max magicule, **undead**. Apex race: **Void Siphon**.

**Ending it**

| How | Result |
|---|---|
| **Rite of Cleansing Blood** | becomes a **siphoner** and leaves the vampire faction |
| **Rite of the Silver Moon** | no effect |
| **The Cure** | becomes human |
| Leaving the vampires another way | becomes a siphoner |

## Tribrids

A **tribrid** is a **witch who is also a werewolf and completes the hybrid transition** (R64). A tribrid is a hybrid with the witch layer:

- everything a [hybrid](Hybrids-And-Original-Hybrids) gets (age traits, blood thirst, venom, sire bond, shifting at will once unbound)
- the **witch bonus**: altar range x1.5, rite cost x0.75
- **siphoning**
- **prey** for hunters; a vampire and a werewolf for every rule (R2)
- Tensura (Tensura part): skills `drain`, `beast_transformation`, `self_regeneration`, `charm`, +30% max magicule, **undead**. Apex race: **Tribrid Ascendant** (the most max health of the seven, by the placeholder stats).
- Needs Werewolves.

**How to become one:** a witch is bitten and becomes a werewolf (see [Werewolves and packs](Werewolves-And-Packs)), an Original makes Original's Blood, the witch-werewolf drinks it and dies, then drinks Doppelgänger Blood in time. See [The transition](Hybrids-And-Original-Hybrids#the-transition).

**Ending it**

| How | Result |
|---|---|
| **Rite of Cleansing Blood** | becomes a **witch** who is still a werewolf (loses the sire bond) |
| **Rite of the Silver Moon** | becomes a plain **vampire** (loses the witch layer) |
| **The Cure** | becomes human |
| Leaving the werewolf faction another way | becomes a witch |

Both rites are for non-Originals only: see [Rites](Rites#species-and-cures).
