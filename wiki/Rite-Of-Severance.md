# Rite of Severance

> **This rite is not in the mod yet.** It is being built right now. This page describes the owner's design (rule **R101b**), so details like the exact ingredients, names and messages **may change**. Don't plan a server around it until it ships.

<!-- verify: severance -->

## What it is

Siring needs no consent: it comes from blood or an Alpha's bite. The Rite of Severance gives a sired player a way out. It **breaks the whole sire bond** (see [Sirelines](Sirelines)).

## What it does

For the **sired** who goes through it:

- they are freed: **no Tensura owner** (the sire stops being their Tensura permanent owner; see [Tensura integration](Tensura-Integration#sires-as-tensura-owners-and-naming))
- they **can hurt their former sire**
- the sire can no longer use **`/bloodline call`** on them
- **compulsion** no longer works on them

What it does **not** do (blood, not bond):

- the **family tree still records the sire**
- **sireline death still applies** to them (see [Sirelines](Sirelines#sireline-death))

## Who can perform it

A **witch type**: a **witch, siphoner, heretic or tribrid**, at an Enchanted altar, with the **sired standing in the circle**.

## Cost

- only on a **full-moon night**
- **6000** altar power
- a **rare ingredient** (not decided)
- **Sire's Blood**: an item **bound to the sire's UUID**. You get it by **hitting the sire with a silver blade while holding a glass bottle in your offhand**.

## Not final

Everything above comes from a design note, not from the code. When the rite is built this page will be checked against it. The panel fixes in the species_tensura part land first, because the rite touches the same files.
