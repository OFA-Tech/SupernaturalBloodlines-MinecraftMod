# For players

A short guide to what you can become, what it costs, and how to stop being it. Your server decides which parts are on: see [Parts and compatibility](Parts-And-Compatibility).

## How do I become...

| I want to be... | Do this | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **A vampire** | Get infected by a vampire (Vampirism). | Vampirism |
| **An Original** | The [Rite of Immortality](Rites#rite-of-immortality): full moon, a villager, white oak sapling, golden apple, human heart. | Vampirism, Enchanted |
| **A hybrid** | A werewolf drinks Original's Blood, dies, then drinks Doppelgänger Blood in time. Or a vampire with the hidden gene kills a villager or a player. | Werewolves |
| **An Original hybrid** | An Original with the gene: [Rite of the Moon](Rites#rite-of-the-moon). | Werewolves |
| **A witch or a siphoner** | [Rite of Ancestral Calling](Rites#rite-of-ancestral-calling) (75% witch, 25% siphoner). | Enchanted |
| **A heretic** | A siphoner who joins the vampires. | |
| **A tribrid** | A witch-werewolf completes the hybrid transition. | Werewolves |
| **A werewolf** | Be bitten by an Alpha (Beta of their pack) or a wild werewolf (Omega), or awaken the hidden gene. | Werewolves |
| **An Alpha** | Steal the spark by killing an Alpha, or inherit it when your Alpha leaves. | Werewolves |
| **A True Alpha** | Max Werewolves level, 30 days as a Beta, 300 defender kills, no innocent kills, no stolen spark. | Werewolves |
| **A Hunter of the Five** | A Vampirism hunter: [Rite of the Five](Rites#rite-of-the-five). | Vampirism, Enchanted |

With **Tensura**, only **human-like races** can take a bloodline, and you **keep your Tensura race**. See [Tensura integration](Tensura-Integration).

## How do I stop being...

| You are | Cure |
|---|---|
| a **vampire** | [Rite of Cleansing Blood](Rites#species-and-cures) |
| a **werewolf** | [Rite of the Silver Moon](Rites#species-and-cures) (full moon) |
| a **hybrid** | Cleansing Blood (plain werewolf), the Silver Moon (plain vampire), The Cure (human) |
| an **Original or Original hybrid** | **Only The Cure** |
| anything else | The Cure removes every bloodline |

The Cure needs a *desiccated* Original: see [Rites](Rites#rite-of-the-cure).

## What am I weak to?

| Weakness | Hurts |
|---|---|
| **White oak** (the stake) | kills Originals at once |
| **White Oak Ash Dagger** | desiccates an Original; hurts any supernatural who wields it |
| **Vervain** (tea, offhand) | vampires who bite or hit you |
| **Mountain ash** lines | vampire and werewolf types can't cross |
| **Wolfsbane**, wolf lichen, silver | werewolf types |
| **Werewolf venom** | vampires: lethal without Hybrid's Blood |
| **Lightning and the Taser** | any supernatural: Shocked |
| **A lunar eclipse** (3% of full moons) | werewolves lose their forms until dawn |
| **Holy damage** (Tensura) | vampire types take x3 |
| **Hunters of the Five** | grow with every kill of you |

## Things you can do

- **Compel** villagers (discount for a day, or follow you) and lesser vampires: **sneak + right-click** with an empty hand, eye to eye, within 4 blocks. Originals, hybrids and vampires of level 10+. See [Sirelines](Sirelines#compulsion).
- **Call your sired:** `/bloodline call` (Originals and hybrids, 10 minute cooldown).
- **Read your sireline:** `/bloodline tree`.
- **Run a pack:** `/pack invite`, `/pack info`, `/pack roar`, `/pack heal`. See [Werewolves and packs](Werewolves-And-Packs#packs-and-pack).
- **Siphon** (siphoner, heretic, tribrid): sneak + right-click a powered block or a creature.
- **Protect yourself:** Sun, Silver and Spirit Poppets; the Resurrection Ring; the Sun Ring and the Moon Ring. See [Items and blocks](Items-And-Blocks).
- **Find vampires:** the Vampire Compass. **Stun supernaturals:** the Hearing Device.
