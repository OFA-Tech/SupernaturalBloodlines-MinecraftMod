# Changelog

## Unreleased: sirelines

- Compulsion: an Original, hybrid, tribrid or a vampire of level 10 or more can sneak and click with an empty hand on a villager or a lesser vampire (not advanced vampires, bosses or converted creatures) within 4 blocks and eye to eye (the target looks within 30 degrees of the caster, the caster within 15 of the target). Players, vervain users and hunters are immune.
- Compelled villager: a 5 second choice in chat, `[Discount]` (a cure-sized discount, major positive gossip 20, for a day; what it added is taken back exactly when it ends, it does not stack, and a villager holds one discount at a time) or `[Follow]` (walks to you for 5 minutes). The buttons run `/bloodline compel <token> discount|follow`.
- Compelled lesser vampire: becomes your sired, does not despawn, and never targets you or anyone below you in your sireline (everyone else is still prey). A vampire already sired by another stays theirs.
- Optional sireline death (`sireline_death` in the species config, default off): when an Original or Original hybrid is killed with white oak, everyone they sired, and everyone those sired, dies too (damage type `supernaturalbloodlines:sireline`, which gets past armor, invulnerability, effects and totems). A sired vampire in Vampirism's dying state is killed too, and a sireline death gives a hunter no mark kill. Offline players die once on their next login (only if they still have a sire), loaded creatures are removed, unloaded ones when their chunk loads (nothing is force-loaded). A sired Original and their line survive. The dead leave the sireline tree.
- `/bloodline call`: a dead sired player cannot accept; if the caller is in the air or in water the sired land on the ground below (up to 8 blocks) and the call is refused when there is none.
- Fixed a flaky GameTest (`senseSendsPreyInRange`: two of its mobs stood outside the test structure).
- Pending (manual): the two-client check of `/bloodline call` and compulsion (chat buttons, the trade screen not opening, a real walk to the caller).

## Unreleased: hunters

- Rite of the Five (a slot counts for a Hunter, a live potential and an owed potential alike; medium circle, 10000 power: 5 candles, iron ingot, book, taglock): turns a hunter-faction human into a Hunter of the Five. The Five are kept in a roster (`max_five` in the species config, default 5); leaving the hunter faction frees the slot, logging out does not.
- Hunter's Mark: a prey kill (vampire and werewolf types; never witches or siphoners) grows the mark, up to 20 kills: +1% attack damage and +0.5% movement speed per kill. A potential who leaves the hunters owes a new one like a lost mark. A marked hunter killed by prey loses the mark, and an online human hunter (random) becomes a potential; with nobody online the choice waits for the next hunter to join. A potential's first prey kill completes the Five. Kill credit survives Vampirism's dying state (a player attacker is found again by UUID).
- Hunter's Curse: a prey killer of a marked hunter hallucinates (phantom zombie, whisper or darkness, every 600 +- 200 ticks, only that player sees it) until a potential completes the Five. It does not stack, and humans are not cursed. Werewolf venom sends the same hallucinations.
- Instinct: a Hunter of the Five with no prey kill for 3 days (72000 ticks; the clock starts when the mark is gained, by any path) gets Weakness I, checked every 1200 ticks, unless on a scoreboard team with an online prey player.
- Vampire sense (5 kills or more): every 40 ticks the server tells the hunter which prey (no witches, siphoners or spectators) are within 24 blocks, and the hunter's client outlines them. Only that client sees it; the server sets no glow.
- `HunterRules.immuneToCompulsion(Player)` (potentials and Hunters of the Five) for the compulsion in plan 8.
- Access transformer `META-INF/accesstransformer.cfg` (`Entity.setSharedFlag` and `getSharedFlag` public) for the client-only outline, which puts back the glow flag it found (a glow that starts while an entity is outlined is lost when the outline ends), because the species part's mixin budget is used.
- Known debts: the outline is the team colour (white without a team); a vampire that dies of the dying state's timer after more than 10 minutes, or whose killer logged out, is no kill; no Vampirism hunter skin on the phantom zombie.
- Pending (manual): the two-client check that the outline is visible only to the hunter; how the hallucinations look in a running client; kill credit through the dying state with a real vampire (kill it, let it give up or time out, and see the mark grow).

## Unreleased: artifacts

- Sun Ring (craft: 4 gold ingots around glowstone; rite *Ward of Daylight*: ring, 2 sunflowers, 4 glowstone dust, medium circle, 6000 power): bound to the vampire on the circle (data component `supernaturalbloodlines:bound_to`). While they wear it (Curios `ring` slot or inventory) Vampirism's sun damage is cancelled, and a hidden sunscreen effect also stops the sun kill and nausea (Vampirism has no sun hook). Weakness in the sun is not stopped. A ring bound to someone else, or unbound, does nothing.
- Moon Ring (needs Werewolves; craft: 4 silver ingots around an amethyst shard; rite *Ward of the Moon*: ring, 2 amethyst shards, 2 silver ingots, medium circle, 6000 power; bound to a werewolf): moon control (R96). The forced full-moon shift is skipped for the owner. Half forms at will, full forms from Werewolves level 8 (`werewolves.full_form_level` in the common config).
- Shared `MoonControl` and `ForcedShift` (package `shared.werewolves`): the hybrids' forced-shift cancelling moved there; hybrids are one control source, the Moon Ring another. No new mixin.
- A rite factory `supernaturalbloodlines:bind_item` (input, output, optional faction): binds the person on the circle to the output item; wrong faction, a missing input or an already bound input refunds the ingredients and says why.
- Curios is optional: the rings are in the tag `curios:ring`; without Curios they work from the inventory.
- Placeholder textures for both rings.
- Vampire Compass (rite *Rite of the Vampire Compass*: compass + vervain, medium circle, 2000 power): the needle points at the nearest vampire, hybrid or Original within 64 blocks (not yourself), refreshed once a second on the server through the vanilla lodestone tracker; with nobody in range it spins. Client: an `angle` item property with 32 placeholder needle frames.
- Hearing Device (rite *Rite of the Hearing Device*: note block, 2 iron ingots, amethyst shard, medium circle, 3000 power): right click stuns every supernatural (vampire, werewolf, species; mob, or a player the user could hit with PvP on and who is not creative or spectating) within 5 blocks for 5 seconds (Slowness V + Nausea), 60 second cooldown. The user, humans and other mobs are not affected.
- Mountain ash: sapling, log, leaves and planks (the tree grows from the sapling and generates rarely in forest biomes through a biome modifier), and *mountain ash dust* (stonecutter: 1 log -> 4 dust). Dust is placed like redstone dust and lays a line that gives no power. Vampires, werewolves, hybrids, Originals, heretics and tribrids cannot walk across it (a 1.5 block wall for them only; witches, siphoners and humans pass), they are stopped by collision only (no path planning), arrows and tridents they fire stick into the wall, other projectiles are discarded on contact, and they cannot break it by hand. The line is not a floor: nothing can be placed or spawn on it. A line flagged as laid by a pack Emissary (the flag exists, plan 9 sets it) also stops the mobs in `#supernaturalbloodlines:supernatural_mobs` (only the Vampirism ghost for now).
- Resurrection Ring (rite *Ward of Return*: totem of undying, 4 gold ingots, diamond; medium circle, 8000 power): for a human-side player (no vampire or werewolf), a death caused by a supernatural attacker is cancelled once per charge (3 charges): full health, Regeneration II for 10 seconds. An empty ring does nothing. Deaths that Vampirism, the Original rules or a Totem already saved cost no charge.
- Vervain: crop (8 ages), seeds (village house chests, 25%), herb and Vervain Tea (2 vervain + sugar in a kettle; 10 minutes of the `vervain` effect). Wearing the effect or holding it in the offhand: vampires who bite you or hit you barehanded take 4 magic damage and Weakness (a vervain bite gives no blood), and vampires who eat it take 6. It is also the compulsion immunity rule for plan 8 (`VervainRules.protects`). A werewolf's bite does not burn (Werewolves has no hook).
- White Oak Ash Dagger (white oak log smelted into ash; the dagger is ash over iron over a stick) and The Cure: a dagger hit on an Original or Original hybrid leaves it in them as the `desiccated` effect (30 minutes: no moving, attacking or using items) until another player pulls it (right click), it times out, is removed or they die. Anyone supernatural who wields it takes 10 damage per hit. *Rite of the Cure* (2 white oak ash, 4 vervain, enchanted golden apple; needs a desiccated Original within 6 blocks) makes The Cure; drinking it removes every layer and the faction (`DRANK_THE_CURE`). A desiccated Original cannot drink it: someone feeds it to them.
- Wolfsbane strains (R95, needs Werewolves; nothing is registered without it): yellow (uncommon) and northern blue (rare) wolfsbane with seeds and a wheat-like crop, wolf lichen (a carpet that generates on stone in cold biomes), and an ash of each (smelt the plant). They only affect werewolf types (werewolf faction, hybrids, Original hybrids, tribrids); for everyone else the plant is just food. Common (purple, Werewolves' and Enchanted's plant, now edible): Weakness I + Nausea 30 s. Yellow: 1 damage every 2 s for 60 s (never kills). Northern blue: 1 damage every 5 s and death after 5 minutes (6000 ticks, damage type `wolfsbane_poison`). Lichen: no natural regeneration and no werewolf form changes (except back to human) for 60 s. Sources: eating the plant, an anvil coating (sword or axe + plant: `wolfsbane_coating`, 8 hits on werewolf types), potions (brew a plant into an awkward potion; splash, lingering and arrows follow). Cure: eat the ash of the same strain, or use it while sneaking; other ashes do nothing. Seeds from chests (yellow 15% in village houses and outposts, northern blue 4% in igloos, dungeons and mineshafts).
- Known debts: a desiccated vampire can still start a Vampirism bite (it drinks nothing, but the victim still takes the bite); no grey screen tint; mountain ash lines are found by prey mobs only through collision (no path planning), and explosions and pistons destroy them; no flammability, leaf tint or potted sapling for mountain ash; the Sun Ring does not stop Weakness in the sun; rings cannot be rebound; wolfsbane: the lichen does not spread, the wild crops do not generate, the common strain has no ash or potion, the coated weapon has no tooltip, and the lichen's regeneration block works by reading the call stack for `FoodData` (a mod that heals through `FoodData.tick` is blocked too).
- Pending (manual, see `docs/parts/artifacts.md`): the Accessories ring slots in the OFA pack, every rite at a real circle, the compass needle and the mountain ash and wolfsbane visuals in a running client, real sunlight for the Sun Ring, a real full-moon shift. The Taser needs the packs part.
- Test template `tall` (16x10x16) for tests whose entities are taller than a block.

## Unreleased: species

- Originals: the Rite of Immortality makes one (needs a full moon, a white oak sapling, a golden apple, a human heart and a villager). +4 max health, +20% attack, and lethal damage that is not white oak, the void or `/kill` cannot kill them (vampires use Vampirism's dying state, Original hybrids a downed state of 30 seconds with a Give up / Wait screen).
- White oak: log, planks, sapling (grows a white oak tree) and the White Oak Stake, the only weapon that kills an Original.
- Original's Blood: Rite of Original's Blood fills a glass bottle (Originals only); drinking it gives the `original_blood` effect, and a werewolf who dies with it in them starts the hybrid transition. The last blood drunk before death is the sire.
- Hybrid transition (24000 online ticks, config): Doppelgänger Blood completes it, any other blood makes it unstable and then kills, running out of time kills. The timer survives a relog and a respawn.
- Hybrid traits: movement speed and attack damage that grow over 50 in-game days (1,200,000 ticks), blood thirst (Weakness at 0, Strength from 15), hybrid shifts at will (the forced full-moon shift is cancelled), and the sire bond (a hybrid cannot hurt their sire, directly or by projectile, cloud or pet).
- Venom and Hybrid's Blood: a werewolf's melee hit on a vampire poisons them for 10 minutes (1 damage pulses, hallucinations, death at the end; Originals are weakened instead); Hybrid's Blood (a rite, one glass bottle) cures it.
- Original hybrid: the Rite of the Moon turns an Original with the werewolf gene into one. Original hybrids cannot be cured.
- The R24 gene kill: a vampire or Original with the hidden gene who kills a villager or a player becomes a bound hybrid; the faction level carries over (capped) when the base faction changes.
- Witches and siphoners: the Rite of Ancestral Calling (25% siphoner by default). Witches widen an altar's range x1.5 and pay x0.75 for rites (mixins into Enchanted, each with `require = 0`, a missing one only turns the bonus off). Siphoners drain altar-power blocks and creatures for siphoned power (cap 1000, reset on death) and can pay a rite's start cost with it.
- Heretics (a siphoner who joins the vampires) keep siphoning and the witch bonus. Tribrids (a witch who completes the hybrid transition) are hybrids with the witch bonus and siphoning. A siphoner cannot start the hybrid transition.
- Hybrid-type species and their rites need Werewolves; without it they are refused and the rites are not loaded.
- Fixed flaky GameTests: the test template was 1x1x1, so cells next to a chunk edge had entities in chunks that do not tick. It is 16x1x16 now.
- Known pending (manual): the two-client playthrough (Original, Original's Blood, a werewolf friend turned hybrid, Original hybrid, witch, tribrid), the forced-shift check on a real full moon, the downed screen, placeholder textures. Moonstone has no loot source yet. The Werewolves bite as a source of thirst and venom is not hooked (no hook exists).

## Unreleased: vampirism_enchanted

- Plant parity (data): Enchanted garlic is in the `c:crops/garlic` tag, so all Vampirism recipes that use garlic (and the cauldron colour) accept it. **Limitation:** the tag is only read by recipes. The garlic crop's effect on vampires and the garlic diffusers do not read it, so an `enchanted:garlic` crop does not hurt a vampire.
- Enchanted wolfsbane converts to and from Werewolves' wolfsbane (two shapeless recipes; Werewolves has no tag): 1 Enchanted flower gives 1 Werewolves wolfsbane, 2 Werewolves wolfsbane give 2 Enchanted flowers (a single wolfsbane is Werewolves' own purple dye recipe, so a 1:1 recipe would collide with it). Only with Werewolves.
- Altar power: Vampirism garlic and vampire orchid, Werewolves wolfsbane and wolf berry bush (8 per block, up to 20).
- Blood data: `entity_blood` entries for the Enchanted familiar cat (3) and mandrake (0).
- A rite factory `supernaturalbloodlines:faction_rite` (event, faction requirement, optional full moon) that applies a bloodline event to the person on the circle through `BloodlineRules`; a failure refunds the ingredients and says why.
- Rite of Cleansing Blood (garlic, holy water, golden apple): cures vampirism. Rite of the Silver Moon (2 silver ingots, wolfsbane, full moon; Werewolves only): cures lycanthropy. Originals and hybrids follow the bloodline rules.
- Rite of Blood Offering: 4 full Vampirism blood bottles and a stone make a `blood_stone` block (altar power 25, up to 16). Empty bottles are not accepted.
- Sun Poppet: protects its bound player from a lethal hit of Vampirism's sun damage. Silver Poppet (Werewolves only, not registered without it): protects from a lethal hit by a silver item (`werewolves:type/silver`), a silver bolt or other silver ammunition shot at the player, or a werewolf bite. Both follow Enchanted's rule (poppets are only consulted on a lethal hit) and are made at the spinning wheel from an Enchanted poppet, anointing paste and glowstone dust or a silver ingot.
- Known pending: manual in-game checks of the rites at a real chalk circle, the poppets at a real spinning wheel, and the placeholder textures (block and poppets).

## Unreleased: enchanted_tensura

- Altar power (data): Tensura magic ore, magic blocks, crystal blocks, magisteel and magic metal blocks power Enchanted altars (6 per ore up to 20, 10 per block up to 10).
- EP data (`entity_existence`) for the Enchanted mandrake and familiar cat. Numbers are first guesses.

- Spirit Poppet: protects the player it is bound to from lethal damage of the `tensura:is_spiritual` types (soul scatter, soul consumed, synthesise), following Enchanted's rule (poppets are only consulted on a lethal hit). Spinning wheel recipe from the Enchanted poppet, a medium magic crystal and soul sand.
- Known pending: manual in-game checks of the rites at a real chalk circle, the curses on a cursed player, the Tensura analysis screen for Enchanted mobs, and the placeholder textures (block and poppet).
## Unreleased: vampirism_tensura

- Unified vampire sync: a Tensura race in the vampire line joins the Vampirism vampire faction at a level floor (`vampire` 1, `vampire_overcomer` and `vampire_lord` 7, `divine_vampire` max; `ghoul` no faction change). The floor never lowers an existing level. Joining Vampirism keeps the Tensura race, except an exact `tensura:human` becomes `tensura:vampire` automatically (R20a); a cure resets the race to `cure_race` (default `tensura:human`) only if it is in the vampire line.
- Single sun burn: Tensura's own vampire burn and sun debuffs are disabled for faction vampires (2 mixins, see `docs/mixins.md`); Vampirism's sun handling wins.
- Hunter and werewolf faction rules by Tensura race tags: `tensura:undead` and `daemon` can't be hunters (and leave the faction if they become one); `undead`, `daemon` and `no_blood` can't be werewolves.
- Tensura holy damage hurts Vampirism vampires x1.5 (`tensura_holy_vs_vampire`). Vampirism holy water already counts as Tensura holy damage.
- Bloodless races can't be bitten: a bite mixin for `tensura:no_blood` race players and an `entity_blood` data map (blood 0) for Tensura bloodless mobs.
- Werewolf skill grants: werewolf levels grant configured Tensura skills (default `tensura:godwolf_sense` at max level).
- EP data (`entity_existence`) for Vampirism and Werewolves mobs. Numbers are first guesses, to tune after playtesting.
- New config file `supernaturalbloodlines-vampirism_tensura.toml`.
- Known pending: manual check in game that the Tensura analysis screen shows the EP of Vampirism and Werewolves mobs (covered only by registry GameTests).
