# Changelog

## Unreleased: balance pass (plan 10)

Owner decisions of 2026-11-14, applied to existing parts. Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-balance-pass-design.md`.

- **Apex races cost 2,000,000 EP** (was 1,000,000), the same as a divine human. Players already on an apex race keep it.
- **The evolution boost speeds up EP gain only.** A player with a species is credited 25% more EP (`evolution_progress_multiplier`, default 1.25, 1.0 = off; the key keeps its old name). It no longer scales the evolution progress, so a boss, an item or an awakening can't be skipped. Done through Tensura's own `AURA_GAIN`/`MAGICULE_GAIN` attributes (Tensura has no EP-gain event): a permanent modifier `supernaturalbloodlines:ep_boost`, refreshed with the species layer. The `getEvolutionProgress` injection of `RaceInstanceMixin` is gone; the class stays for the apex offer (still 3 mixins in the part). The setting is server-side only now. A player who had the old progress bonus loses it; EP already gained is kept.
- **No apex race is bloodless.** All seven carry the new race tag `supernaturalbloodlines:bitable`. The vampire bite block and the bloodless part of the werewolf-join check skip it, so every apex race can be bitten. The vampire-type ones stay undead (holy damage unchanged). Tensura's own drain and blood mist still skip an undead apex race (Tensura's check, left alone).

- **True Alphas keep the spark.** Killing a True Alpha is just a death: nobody steals the spark and the victim stays True Alpha. A True Alpha who is cured or leaves the werewolves stays True Alpha, dormant while they are not a werewolf (no attack or health perk, no `/pack invite|heal|emissary`; the Tensura rank skills still follow the rank, as before). Their pack dissolves as for any Alpha, no Beta inherits, and the pack is not restored when they rejoin; the perks come back at once. A True Alpha cannot give up the spark with `/pack heal` (`true_alpha_keeps_spark`). A plain Alpha is unchanged. Saves: only events after the update follow this; a True Alpha already made an Omega by an earlier cure stays an Omega.

- **Werewolves follow Tensura's Beastfolk line (R20b, vampirism_tensura, needs Werewolves).** A player whose Tensura race is exactly `tensura:human` becomes `tensura:beastfolk` when they become a werewolf (as R20a does for vampires). An Alpha already on the line is a `beast_lord`, a True Alpha a `spirit_beast`. The race is never lowered, `divine_beast` stays a Tensura evolution, and every other race (an elf, a vampire-raced hybrid, any player with a bloodline species) is kept. Config `werewolf_beastfolk_enabled` (default on, in `supernaturalbloodlines-vampirism_tensura.toml`). Existing werewolves keep their race: only a join or a rank change after the update counts.

## Unreleased: species_tensura (species layer)

Needs Vampirism, Enchanted and Tensura (and the species part). Details in `docs/parts/species_tensura.md`.

- A species is a layer on the player's Tensura race (the race is never replaced): skills (granted free of magicule cost and tracked, so only those are ever taken back), +% max magicule/aura (`supernaturalbloodlines:layer_magicule` / `layer_aura`) and Tensura's undead treatment. Defaults and the config are in `supernaturalbloodlines-species_tensura.toml` (`layers`, `undead_treatment_enabled`). It is a common config: keep it equal on the server and the clients.
- **Owner balance point (please rule on it):** a species whose layer says undead, and a plain Vampirism vampire (`VAMPIRE` entry), now count as undead for Tensura. This is wider than R100's "holy damage and sunlight":
  - a plain faction vampire goes from x1.5 (`tensura_holy_vs_vampire`) to Tensura's x3 holy and light damage; `tensura_holy_vs_vampire` has no effect on a vampire whose layer is undead (the two multipliers would stack to x4.5, so the bridge steps aside, and only when the mixin on `RaceUtils` really applied);
  - Tensura's other undead rules apply too: infection immunity, Enervation on food, holy exposure, and the rest of what Tensura asks `RaceUtils.isUndead` / `isAffectedByHolyExposure`;
  - options: keep it (default), switch it off with `undead_treatment_enabled=false` (skills and bonuses stay, the bridge's x1.5 returns), or narrow the `treatAsUndead` flags of the VAMPIRE / species entries in `layers`.
- R103: the exemption from the undead/daemon denial for hunters and werewolves only holds for an apex race (race id in the `supernaturalbloodlines` namespace) of a vampire-type species (Original, Original hybrid, Hybrid, Heretic, Tribrid). An Original with a Tensura ghoul race is ejected from the hunters like anyone else.
- A skill two sources grant (godwolf_sense from the layer and from the werewolf max level) is kept until the last source lets go (`shared/tensura/SkillOwners`); a skill the player had before is never taken.
- Human-like for species (plan 04's check): the `tensura:human_like` tag only has the five base races, so the evolved races of those lines count too.
- Apex evolutions: from saint tier of a human-like Tensura line, a player with a species may pick one apex race in Tensura's evolution screen (`primordial_vampire`, `primordial_hybrid`, `moonblood_hybrid`, `ancestral_witch`, `void_siphon`, `tribrid_ascendant`, `eternal_hunter`; names, stats and signature skills are placeholders). Config: `apex_evolutions_enabled`, `evolution_progress_multiplier` (1.25).
- **Warning before removing the mod:** reset players on an apex race with Tensura's race command first. ManasCore throws for a saved race that is not registered, so such a player cannot load at all. With only `species_tensura` switched off the races stay registered and nothing breaks; an Original hunter on `primordial_vampire` is then ejected from the hunters (R103 has no exemption without the part).
- **Warning before rolling back:** rolling back to a build before `species_tensura`: first reset apex-race players with Tensura's race command; back up the world before the first deploy. An older build also forgets severances (Rite of Severance), so severed bonds come back there.
- **Skills granted by this mod stay when the part is switched off:** the layer, rank and sire-link code only runs while `species_tensura` is on, so skills it already granted (and their tracking) stay on the player, and so do the sire links and their one-way friendly fire, until the part is on again or they are removed by hand. To clean up first: remove the species from the players (or set the `layers` / `rank_skills` entries to empty and let everyone log in), then switch the part off.
- **Owner decision (naming):** `sire_naming_enabled` (default on) lets a sire name their own sired even with Tensura's `playerNaming` game rule off. The server rule does not beat that exception; it bypasses the rule for a sire and their own sired only, nobody else. Siring needs no consent (it comes from blood or an Alpha bite).
- **Config mismatch (known gap):** the apex option is added in `getNextEvolutions` on the client and the server. If `apex_evolutions_enabled` differs (on for the client, off for the server), the client shows the apex race and the server silently refuses the pick. A refusal cannot be told apart from a plain query of the list without a fourth mixin on Tensura's evolution packet (the part's budget is three), so nothing is logged: keep the common config equal on both sides.
- **Owner point (evolution bonus):** `evolution_progress_multiplier` scales Tensura's weighted TOTAL progress, not each requirement. At 1.25 up to 20% of the requirement weight can stay unmet, so non-EP conditions (a boss, an item, an awakening) can be skipped once the EP part is high enough. It applies to every evolution of a player with a species. Set it to 1.0 to turn it off. Common config: keep the client and server values equal.
- **Owner point (bloodless):** the four vampire-type apex races (all but `void_siphon`, which is not undead) are tagged `tensura:undead`, and Tensura's `no_blood` includes `#tensura:undead`, so they are bloodless too: nobody can bite them. That reaches past the Original (R100 says bloodless for Originals): hybrids, original hybrids and tribrids get it as well.
- **Owner point (balance):** an apex race needs 1,000,000 EP (a divine human needs 2,000,000) and gives more max health (1000 against 980) than the divine tier: cheaper and stronger. All values are placeholders.
- **Owner point (mastery):** when a species is lost, its layer skills go dormant (forgotten, tracked) and come back when it returns, but the mastery of those skills is lost. Keeping it needs a new per-skill field in the bloodline state (the codec is at its 16-field cap).
- Sireline as Tensura subordinates (R101): the sire becomes the Tensura permanent owner of the sired (players while they are supernatural, creatures, tamed when they are `ISubordinate` and the sire is online) and can name them through Tensura's menu, even with `playerNaming` off, if the sired switched their own naming on. A sired player named this way gets Tensura's stats but never its race evolution. Config: `sire_subordinate_link_enabled`, `sire_naming_enabled`. Third and last mixin of the part (`SireNamingMixin`). A sire naming a sired player is told in chat that it grants EP and stats only, never an evolution.
- **Owner point (friendly fire):** as Tensura subordinates, a sired player (a werewolf-bitten one too, the sire is the Alpha) cannot hurt their sire or the sire's other sired through Tensura's hurt handler; the sire can still hurt them. `sire_subordinate_link_enabled=false` removes it. A demon lord sire's harvest festival also gifts its sired.
- **Owner point (Tensura skills):** Starved and Usurper do not check for subordinates, so a sire can drain max EP, plunder or rob skills from a sired player with them, and Usurper's takeover masks the sire as owner while it lasts (bytecode only). Nothing was changed; say if sireline members should be exempt.
- **Removing the links:** set `sire_subordinate_link_enabled=false` and have everyone log in once, then disable the part. With the part off nothing runs and the links stay.
- Werewolf rank skills (Werewolves only): an Alpha gets `beast_transformation` and `godwolf_sense`, a True Alpha also `commander`, a born werewolf of level 5 gets `godwolf_sense`. Config `rank_skills` (`"RANK=skill,skill"`; a bad entry is ignored, logged at ERROR and told to ops in chat on login). The race never changes; only skills this mod granted are taken back, and a skill another source (the species layer, the werewolf level grants) still wants stays (`SkillOwners`). The rank skills do not need the werewolf faction: a cured Alpha keeps the rank and its skills (the attribute perks go).
- **Owner point (checks):** apex races stay registered whenever Tensura is present, even with `species_tensura` off, because ManasCore has no fallback for an unregistered saved race (NPE in `Entity.load`; see the removal warning above). So the "turn the part off with an apex player" check is: the player loads, the apex race stays, the layer does nothing.
- Checks run: client launched with Vampirism, Werewolves, Enchanted, Tensura, Curios; no error or warning from this mod, `species_tensura` mixin config applied, `RaceInstanceMixin` and `RaceUtilsMixin` report "hooks in place" and `SireNamingMixin` applies (no "hook is not in place" warning). All GameTests pass with all mods, `-PnoWerewolves -PnoCurios`, `-PnoLinkedMods`, `-PpartsOff=species` and `-PpartsOff=speciesTensura`.
- Pending (manual, in a real client): an elf taken to the Original apex offer in the evolution screen; a human to the witch apex; a sire naming a sired player through the real naming menu; an Alpha getting the skills in the skill list; the evolution screen, skill list and analysis screen of a layered player; the race icon of the apex races (no texture exists).

- Spark, True Alpha, ranks and eyes: see the next section.
- Pack bonus: with 3 or more Betas within 64 blocks of an online Alpha, +5% attack per Beta counted (bitten Betas twice, up to +25%) and Regeneration I for those Betas. An offline or distant Alpha keeps the pack but not the bonus.
- Alpha Roar (unbound keybind "Alpha Roar", `/pack roar`, 60 s): pack Betas within 32 blocks get Strength I, Speed I and may shift at will for 30 s; other werewolves within 16 blocks drop their form and get Slowness II for 5 s. The roarer's eyes glow.

- Moon control (R96, `moon_bound_werewolves` default on): a werewolf shifts at will only with control (Alpha and True Alpha, the Moon Ring, an unbound hybrid; a born wolf with an anchor has half). Anchors (`#supernaturalbloodlines:anchor` items, or a player named with `/pack anchor`) halve the forced shift.
- Lunar eclipse: each full-moon night has `lunar_eclipse_chance` (default 0.03, rolled once per night at dusk when someone is online) of being an eclipse, until dawn. The server announces start and end, the overworld's fog and horizon turn red, and werewolves lose every form action (the full moon's shift too; a form already on is dropped within a second), the Alpha perks (the Omega penalty stays) and the pack bonus, and nobody can roar. - Electricity: a lightning strike on a supernatural, or within 3 blocks of one, gives Shocked (30 s: Slowness I, no natural regeneration, no Werewolves form actions). The Taser (durability 16, repaired with redstone in an anvil; crafted from a lightning rod over redstone over an iron ingot) shocks a supernatural it hits; every hit spends a charge. - Owner design point: with the artifacts part off, a bitten (non-Alpha) wolf has no way to shift at will (R96 needs control and there is no Moon Ring); only the full moon moves it. Say if you want a fallback (for example an anchor or the beast shift at the highest level).
- Pending (manual, two real clients): a pack of 1 Alpha and 3 Betas (the bonus and its Regeneration), the roar (buff, submit, eye glow seen by the other client), the eyes, and the eclipse sky (`/supernaturalbloodlines debug eclipse start`: red fog and horizon, the announcement, the form lock on a real werewolf). `runClient` loaded the mod with no error from it.
