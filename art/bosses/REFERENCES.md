## Owner's reference images (described)
- **Deucalion, human:** short swept dirty-blond hair; a dark knit sweater with a lighter grey textured shawl collar and shoulder panels; dark, intense look. Teen Wolf season 3. Our existing coat/cane/glasses version is wrong: use this sweater look. **Corrected in round 7:** short dark-brown hair and dark stubble, blind (pale clouded eyes), dark glasses in human form; not blond, not blue-eyed. The sweater and collar stay.
- **Deucalion, Demon Wolf:** blue-grey skin (not fur), red glowing eyes, pointed ears, a heavy brow ridge and a wrinkled snout, a full mouth of sharp fangs, dark hair swept back. A gargoyle/demon face rather than a furry wolf.
- **Peter Hale, Alpha form (season 1):** a huge four-legged beast, black and dark blue-grey with a shaggy black mane over the shoulders and back, red glowing eyes, a wolf-like snarling face, long dark limbs and white claws. Moves on all fours.
- **Peter Hale, human:** slicked-back dark hair, a short beard, a camel/tan wool overcoat over a dark grey herringbone waistcoat and a white open-collar shirt.
- **Peter Hale, burned or risen:** at his resurrection he is covered in dark mud and soil, with pale skin showing through. Use this for the burned/risen variant: charred and dirt-streaked, not neat scars.
- **Mikael Mikaelson:** short slicked grey-blond hair, light stubble, pale blue eyes; an all-black outfit (black jacket over a black shirt, black belt with a buckle). He holds the white oak stake upright: a pale silver-white carved stake. NOT Viking leathers or a beard. Our current Mikael is wrong.

## Round 5 findings (agent research)
- **Sebastien Valet** is a French soldier of the 1760s (Seven Years' War, returned to Gévaudan; actor Gilles Marini), not a vampire-like aristocrat: healthy tan skin, dark natural hair, greyish-white infantry coat with coloured cuffs, tricorn. Sources: tvline.com Gilles Marini casting item, yahoo.com Maid of Gévaudan recap, thesupernaturalfoxsisters.com recap, fortticonderoga.org and militaryheritage.com on French uniforms.
- **Peter Hale burned:** third-degree burns over 75% of his body (teen-wolf-pack wiki), face burned on the left side, "burned from head to toe" in Season 6. Charred black, red-raw and mottled scar tissue; mud only as a light film.

Sources call him a soldier, not a nobleman.

## Round 7 findings (owner's corrections)
- **Deucalion is played by Gideon Emery:** short dark-brown hair, dark stubble, blind. Stills: `File:6x19 Deucalion.png` (hair, stubble), `File:3x10 Alpha Pack Deucalion Kali.jpg`, `File:3x01 Deucalion and Scott 2.jpg`, `File:3x07 Deucalion cane blade.jpg` (dark glasses with thin frames), `File:5x14 Deucalion 5.png` (clouded eyes). Wiki: "his eyes were cloudy with a red ring around his iris"; his roar "shattered the lenses of his sunglasses". So the glasses are supported, and they sit alone on the hat layer so they can be hidden.
- **Burned Peter is scarred and charred, not bloody:** the 1x04 and 1x09 stills show healed, ridged, dull pink-brown scar tissue with a waxy shine. Char is grey-black and flaking; raw red only in small splits.
