# Item art references

Research notes behind the colours and materials. Fandom pages return 403 to page fetches, so most facts come from the search snippets of the pages listed (marked **snippet**). "Confirmed" = a source states the look. "Judgement" = no source, a choice that fits the lore and keeps the item readable.

## Rings
- **Sun Ring (Daylight Ring).** Antique/sterling silver band, oval blue **lapis lazuli** cabochon, Salvatore crest engraved around the stone. Confirmed by replica listings of the show prop: https://bazaraki.com/adv/6746282_the-vampire-diaries-damon-salvatore-fashion-ring, https://thesterlinggirls.com/collections/vampire-diaries-lapis-silver-daylight-rings/products/damon-salvatore-daylight-ring-sterling-silver, https://annablasco.com/Daylight-Ring-Blue-Lapis-Lazuli-Stone-Antique-Silver-Finish/613111; reddit note that the stone was named as lapis on screen: https://www.reddit.com/r/TheVampireDiaries/comments/srqgh9/has_anyone_else_ever_noticed_that_all_of_the/. Used: silver `#d4dae4/#9aa3b4`, lapis `#1f3a9a` with gold pyrite flecks `#e8c850`.
- **Moon Ring (Moonlight ring/amulet).** "The stones were placed on silver rings" (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Moonlight_Amulet. The moonstone is "a very pale white color in the show", milky quartz (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Moonstone, https://screenrant.com/the-vampire-diaries-most-iconic-props. Used: silver band, milky white stone `#eef2f6` with a pale blue shade `#c4d2e6`. Exact ring shape not confirmed (judgement: round cabochon).
- **Resurrection Ring (Gilbert Ring).** Ordinary-looking heirloom silver ring "affixed with a black stone"; both rings were made by Scott Kay (Tribal Hollywood line), sterling silver with engraved setting and **black onyx** (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Enchanted_Objects, https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Gilbert_Ring, https://www.amazon.ae/Scott-Kay-UnKaged-Silver-Gilbert/dp/B0178IVZ6Y. Used: engraved silver setting, onyx `#14141c` (horizontal bezel, Jeremy's version). **Gold-toned was checked and not confirmed:** every source found says sterling silver with black onyx (https://bradfordsauction.com/auction-lot/scott-kay-sterling-silver-black-onyx-gilbert-ring_45c4866bf5, https://www.amazon.ae/Scott-Kay-UnKaged-Silver-Gilbert/dp/B0178IVZ6Y). The "freakishly large gold ring with an embedded black gem" in the same wiki list is the **Illusion Ring**, a different object (https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Enchanted_Objects). So the Resurrection Ring stays silver, with a heavier band, a wide bezel and a big onyx. Icon rework (v3): the hole is shrunk and each stone fills the top: sun = lapis oval with a tiny gold crest, moon = round milky stone with a blue-white sheen, resurrection = wide onyx on a heavy dark-silver band.

## Weapons and tools
- **White Oak Stake / Mikael's Stake.** White oak is pale wood; the stake's hilt was "carved into the same shape as the White Oak Ash Daggers" (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Oak_Stake. Used: pale cream `#e2d6bc` with darker grain `#b8aa8c`, point `#968a70`. Mikael's version adds a gold collar, leather grip and red pommel gem (judgement, marks it as the boss reward).
- **White Oak Ash Dagger / Silver Dagger.** "A simple thin silver dagger with an intricately designed hilt", dipped in white oak ash (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Oak_Ash_Daggers, https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Dagger. Used: silver blade `#d4dce6`, pewter hilt with swept quillons and grey ash dusting `#cdcbc2`. Plain Silver Dagger: same blade, plain dark grip.
- **Taser.** Teen Wolf hunters carry an electrified baton / "taser wand" (**snippet**): http://teenwolfwiki.com/Tasers, https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter. Used: black baton with grey grip ribs, steel two-prong head, cyan arc. Exact prop colours not confirmed.
- **Hearing Device.** Nearest show object: the hunters' **ultrasonic emitter** that herds werewolves with a high-pitched sound (**snippet**): https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/Hunters%27_Code, https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter. **Look not confirmed**: no page describes its colours; modelled as a dark casing with a silver horn, antenna and red LED.
- **Valet Pike.** "A wooden staff that had a steel fleur-de-lis-shaped tip", steel mixed with mountain ash and wolfsbane, forged with Valet blood (**snippet**): https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/Marie-Jeanne_Valet, https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/Marie-Jeanne%27s_pike, https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/The_Beast. Used: dark ash shaft with leather grip, **steel** leaf blade etched with violet wolfsbane, steel fleur-de-lis petals (round 1 had gold prongs; changed to steel).
- **Vampire Compass (Gilbert Compass).** "Well, it's not just a watch. It's a sort of compass, but it points to vampires" (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Gilbert_Compass. Used: antique brass pocket-watch case `#b8903c`, parchment face `#e6dcc2`, red needle tip. Case colour is a judgement from "watch"; no photo seen.

## Plants and powders
- **Vervain.** Real *Verbena officinalis*: lobed toothed leaves, spikes of small **mauve/lilac** two-lipped flowers: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbena_officinalis, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verbena. In the show it grew at the white oak's base (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Vervain. Used: green stem, lilac spikes `#b48ad8/#d4b4f0`. Vervain Tea: pale green-gold tea in a white cup (judgement).
- **Wolfsbane strains.** *Aconitum napellus* has hooded blue-purple flowers; *Aconitum vulparia* (wolf's bane) straw-yellow: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aconitum, https://herbaria.plants.ox.ac.uk/bol/plants400/Profiles/AB/Aconitum, https://sites.evergreen.edu/plantchemeco/wolfsbane-fictious-plant-contains-very-real-dangers. Teen Wolf uses purple wolfsbane and yellow wolfsbane (cures Gerard): https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/File:5x19_Chris_and_Gerard_see_the_beast.gif. Used: yellow `#e8cc30`, northern blue `#3a4ac0` (judgement: the show's "northern blue" is a Werewolves-mod strain), palmate leaves, hooded flowers. Seeds and ash reuse the strain colour on neutral grey ash/brown seeds.
- **Wolf Lichen.** Real "wolf lichen" is *Letharia vulpina*, chartreuse yellow-green on bark (general knowledge; no URL fetched). Used: `#d8e450/#b4c82c/#8aa020` on a dark twig.
- **Mountain Ash.** In the show it is a **black** powder made from the ashes of rowan wood (**snippet**): https://www.reddit.com/r/TeenWolf/comments/1tv57hp/how_mountain_ash_is_black_in_tje_show, http://teenwolfwiki.com/mountain_ash. Used: `#2c2c34/#4a4a52`. Round 1 was a light grey pile (wrong).
- **White Oak Ash.** Ash of the burned white oak tree (**snippet**): https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Oak_Ash_Daggers. Used pale grey `#ecebe6/#c2c2bc` (judgement for the shade).

## Vials and boss items
- **The Cure.** "A liquid potion in a small vial" (https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Cure, **snippet**); Katherine's package was "a blood colored vial in a small velvet jewelry box" (http://my.xfinity.com/blogs/tv/2013/03/29/the-vampire-diaries-the-cure, **snippet**). Used: slim vial, deep red liquid `#a0101c`, silver cap with red wax. Round 1 had a wide bottle with a brown cork.
- **Blood vials (Original, Doppelgänger, Hybrid, Sire's, Klaus's).** Blood is red; the old green/blue/pink liquids were wrong. Types are told apart by cork, neck band and flecks (judgement): Original silver cork + gold band + gold flecks; Doppelgänger brown cork + teal band; Hybrid dark cork + amber band + amber (wolf-eye) flecks; Sire's black cork + violet band; Klaus's brown cork + gold band + gold flecks.
- **Moonstone.** Milky, translucent, "looked like a bar of soap" (**snippet**): https://screenrant.com/the-vampire-diaries-most-iconic-props, https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Moonstone. Used: milky white `#eceeea`, blue-grey shade `#b4c2cc`.
- **Esther's Grimoire.** Dark brown leather-bound book (replica listings, **snippet**): https://www.etsy.com/listing/4564610305/esthers-grimoire-o-original-witch. The linked-rings emblem (Linking Spell) is the mod's own idea.
- **Kol's Grimoire, The Elixir, The True Cure, Klaus's blood label, Memory Claws, Mantle of the Beast.** Mod inventions from the boss design doc; **no show look to confirm**. Elixir: Qetsiyah's immortality elixir exists in the show (https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Elixir) but its look is not described in snippets; used a gold liquid in a round flask.
- **Alpha / True Alpha sparks.** Alpha eyes are **red**, Betas gold/yellow, blue after an innocent kill; Scott's True Alpha eyes are also red (**snippet**): https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/Supernatural_Eyes, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSWEfwcAXSA. Round 1 made the True Alpha spark blue (wrong). Now: Alpha red; True Alpha red with a gold iris ring and gold flare; NPC spark stages go gold (Beta) -> amber -> orange-red as it grows.
- **Poppets (Sun, Silver, Spirit).** They come from the Enchanted mod, not the shows; **no show look**. Small cloth dolls with a stitched mouth and a symbol on the chest: gold (sun), silver-grey, pale spirit blue.
