# The Beast of Gévaudan — boss research and design proposal

Status: **proposal for the owner**. Nothing here is built. All numbers are drafts for `docs/balance.md`.
Extends section 7 of `2026-11-16-bloodlines-bosses-design.md` (show bosses, approach A) with a ninth boss.

## 0. Correction to the brief

The Beast of Gévaudan is a **season 5 (second half)** story, not season 6. It is revived in 5A, unmasked in 5B (episodes "Maid of Gévaudan", "The Beast of Beacon Hills", "Apotheosis") and killed in "Apotheosis". Season 6 only shows it in flashbacks (the night that made Tamora Monroe a hunter, in "Face-to-Faceless").

I found **no skull mask on the Beast**. The masks in this storyline belong to the **Dread Doctors** (plague-doctor style masks, one made by the Surgeon for Valack). The Beast's face is feline with an oversized lipless mouth and white-blue eyes. If "skull mask" was meant for the **Anuk-Ite** (season 6), that is a different creature and not covered here. See open question 1.

## 1. Lore summary

Sources (all Teen Wolf Wiki, CC-BY-SA):
- S1 = https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/Sebastien_Valet
- S2 = https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/The_Beast_of_Gevaudan (mirror of the "Beast of Gévaudan" article; the main-site article returned 403)
- S3 = https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/Marie-Jeanne_Valet (mirror; main site 403)
- S4 = web search snippet of https://teen-wolf-pack.fandom.com/wiki/Marie-Jeanne%27s_pike (page itself 403): "Marcel stole the special pike ... and reforged it into a ..." (sword-cane, per S2)

### Origin: Sebastien Valet
- A French soldier born in the early 1700s, serving in the Seven Years' War in North America with his comrade **Marcel**. Running from British soldiers on a rainy night, he fell and drank rainwater from a puddle made by a **wolf's paw print**. That turned him, not a bite (S1, S2).
- Jeff Davis said Sebastien was already evil before the change and the Beast amplified it (S1 editor's note).
- Marcel covered up the killings. The legend began before they got home to Gévaudan, where a boy, Émile, was killed (S2, S3).

### Marie-Jeanne Valet (his sister)
- Best hunter in Gévaudan, with a longbow and crossbow. She led the hunt, not believing in the supernatural until Henri Argent saved her with a mountain ash line (S3).
- She found Marcel's cellar full of bodies, saw Marcel's hand not healing, and worked out that Sebastien was the Beast. She had tested the tavern with **mistletoe-laced wine**, and Sebastien's eyes showed (S3).
- With Henri she forged a **pike** with a fleur-de-lis tip: steel mixed with **wolfsbane and mountain ash**, forged with **her own blood (shared with Sebastien) under a full moon** (S2, S3). Hunted him three years (1764–1767), killed him in 1767, then imposed **damnatio memoriae** (burned everything bearing his name). She married Henri and became the progenitor of the Argent hunters (S3).

### The 1760s history
- Officially 113 victims; the Dread Doctors say about 500 (S1). It targeted the head and neck, never ate its victims, and killed for sport (S1).
- Witness descriptions varied: red-furred giant wolf, cloven-hoofed panther the size of a horse. Argent lore: **oil-black, solid and shapeless at once, a shadow pretending to be real** (S1, S2).

### Abilities
- A werewolf's powers, greatly heightened; the most powerful werewolf on the show. It beat the Hellhound (Parrish) in a fight, and Deucalion treated it as a threat (S2).
- **Shadow wrapping:** it transforms by wrapping shadows around its human body, keeps its clothes (bloody paw prints turned into shoe prints). The "werewolf" label is partly nominal; its powers are more mystical (S2).
- **Size and look:** about twice an Alpha's beast form, more fur, large red visible veins, feline face, lipless oversized mouth, eyes glowing white-blue instead of red, blue or gold, smoke-like black aura (S2).
- Could not be killed by ordinary steel (Sebastien's own words) (S2).

### Weaknesses
- The only effective weapon: a **spear of steel, wolfsbane (or mistletoe) and mountain ash, with Valet blood, forged under a full moon, wielded by a young woman** (S1, S2). The wolfsbane and ash **disable its healing**, then it bleeds black blood (S3).
- **Mountain ash** blocks it (S2, S3). **Mistletoe** reveals it (S3).
- **Kanima venom did not work** (Theo tried) (S2).
- The resurrected Beast could still be killed by the same pike (S2).

### Dread Doctors link
- Marcel wanted his friend back, studied the supernatural, and with two other scientists became the **Dread Doctors**. Their mission: a vessel for the Beast (S2).
- They made a dozen chimeras, and only **Mason Hewitt** worked (a chimera who absorbed his twin; "corrupting something perfectly good" was needed). They put the Beast's spirit in him (S2).
- Mason is human by day and unaware. The Doctors force the change by **broadcast frequencies**; the Beast regains Sebastien's memories over time and the host would be pushed out ("damnatio memoriae" messages left to jog his memory) (S1, S2).
- When the Beast forms around Mason with no frequency it kills two Dread Doctors (S1).
- Marcel kept the pike and reforged it into a sword-cane (S4, S2).

### How it ended in the show
- Sebastien is revealed in Mason's place, hunts Beacon Hills for the pike, and in the fight with Scott calls Scott's memory of Allison "Marie-Jeanne" (she looked like her ancestor). The distraction lets **Lydia (banshee) pull his spirit out of Mason**. The spirit, a black smoke cloud, is held by Parrish and **impaled by Scott with the pike (sword-cane)**, killing it for good (S1, S2).

## 2. Boss design proposal

### Encounter trigger
Two linked parts, both once per world:

1. **The Host.** A harmless-looking NPC villager-type (named, player-model skin) that spawns **once per world**, in a village, after a configurable day count and on a **full moon night**. By day it is human and passive. Spawn chance: **draft 25 % per qualifying full moon, in a world where some player is an Alpha, True Alpha or Hunter of the Five**, until it has appeared. (Show: the Doctors needed a pack's attention and a perfect host.)
2. **Forcing the change.** On later full moon nights the Host transforms. Not an Alpha spark event: this is the Beast's own change.
   - Optionally a player can force it earlier with a rite (open question 2), which is the "Dread Doctors' frequency".

Show accuracy: the Beast is dormant in a human host by day (S2), forced to shift at night by a trigger (S1, S2), hunts for the pike (S1).

### The pike (kill method), available before the fight
- **Rite of the Valet Pike** (Enchanted rite, full moon only): steel (iron ingots), **northern blue wolfsbane** (or any strain, draft), **mountain ash dust**, and the performer's **own blood** (Sire's Blood-style bound bottle). Result: **Valet Pike**, a spear-type weapon.
- The blood is the performer's own (show: Marie-Jeanne's blood, shared with the Beast; Scott, no relation, used it later). Whether it should need hunter blood is open question 5.
- It is **never obtained from the boss**, so the player can plan for the fight. Hunters of the Five know the recipe (a hunter skill or journal entry; draft).

### Phases
| Phase | Form | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Dormant | Human Host NPC | Day: passive and peaceful. Do not kill it: its death (by anyone) fails the encounter until the next full moon (draft: the Beast does not spawn, a Host respawn is allowed once; see question 4). Clue trail: bloody **paw prints that become shoe prints** by day, victims with head and neck wounds. |
| 1 Stalking | Beast (shadow) | At night, from the Host, the Beast kills villagers and hunts a nearby player. Leaves a smoke trail. Not yet in boss HP range. Players with Hunter sense see it from far. |
| 2 The Beast | Beast (full size) | Boss fight. Roar, shadows, regeneration, pack fear. Mountain ash lines hold it. |
| 3 Exposed | Host collapses, spirit leaves | At **25 % health**, a non-pike hit cannot kill the Beast. It collapses to the **Host (1 HP, protected, invulnerable)** and the **Spirit of Gévaudan**, a smoke entity, comes out. |
| 4 Spirit | Smoke cloud | **Only the Valet Pike can hurt it**. It flees through the shadows (fast, flying) for 60 s (draft). If the pike lands, the Beast dies for good. If the timer ends, it returns to a new Host, and the encounter goes back to phase 0 with 50 % health the next full moon (once; question 4). |

Show mapping: phase 3 is "Lydia pulls the spirit out of Mason", with the Host surviving; phase 4 is Parrish holding the smoke and Scott with the pike. We have no banshee, so the exposure is **a damage threshold plus a mountain ash circle** (phase 3 only starts if the Beast is inside or within a mountain ash line, draft; otherwise it just keeps fighting at 25 % with no regen).

### Abilities mapped to mechanics
| Show ability | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| Giant beast form (twice an Alpha) | **Model scale about 2×** the Werewolves model, larger hitbox. Black smoke particles, white-blue eyes (texture). |
| Roar | **Beast Roar:** 40-tick wind-up. Players within 16 blocks get Weakness II and Darkness for 6 s. Non-pack hostile mobs flee. NPC wolves (plan 15) of **Omega/Beta** rank panic. **Alphas and True Alpha holders are immune to the fear** (they stand against it; the show's Alpha pack chooses to fight). Cooldown 20 s (draft). |
| Pack control | None. It is no Alpha and has no pack (the show's Beast is alone). NPC packs avoid it. This keeps Deucalion's Alpha pack separate. |
| Regeneration | Heals about 2 HP/s. **Suppressed by any wolfsbane strain** (coated weapon hit, potion). **Only the pike removes it for good.** Northern blue wolfsbane does **not** kill it (it is not a normal werewolf), it only stops healing. |
| Shadow body | Smoke aura: ranged attacks (arrows, tridents) do **-40 %** (draft). Melee and the pike are not reduced. |
| Strength | Heavy melee, knockback, and a **leap** at range. |
| Resists | **Immune to Kanima-style paralysis** (if added). **Hearing Device stun reduced to 1 s** (draft, vs 5 s for others). Shifts clothing intact (cosmetic only). |
| Mountain ash | **Blocked by an unbroken line** (it is a werewolf type). It cannot break it. Config: `beast_respects_ash`, default on. |
| Dread Doctors | No Doctors in the mod. Their "frequency" is replaced by the optional forcing rite (question 2). |
| Hunter link | **Hunters of the Five sense it** like a supernatural mark from far away; slaying it gives a **large mark/rung boost** (draft). The Monroe hook: a Hunter of the Five who survives its Roar gets a short buff (flavour, optional). |

### Who is meant to fight it
- **Alphas, True Alphas and hunters** with a pack or partners; Omegas and Betas are meant to flee the Roar.
- Mid to late game: gear of a **True Alpha-level player** (900 HP `alpha_primeval` is the yardstick).
- One player can win with preparation (pike, ash lines, wolfsbane-coated weapon), but it is balanced for 2–4 players.

### Unique reward
**Mantle of the Beast** (artifact, one per world), the shadow mantle:
- For a werewolf type: while shifted, wraps you in shadows (smoke particles), and gives **Shadow Wrap**: 20 s of Fear aura (Weakness and Slowness for non-pack enemies within 6 blocks) every 120 s (draft). Your shift keeps gear (cosmetic and, where the shift would otherwise drop items, kept).
- For others: a cosmetic cloak with no effect (so it is a trophy, never a farm item).
- **Marie-Jeanne's Pike stays craftable**; it is not the reward.
- It is **not an Alpha spark** and gives no stacked spark, so it does not touch `max_stacked_sparks` or Deucalion's 3+ rule.

## 3. Tech notes (approach A)

- **Host** (phases 0 and 1): a humanoid NPC on the player model with a custom skin, like the other show bosses. Cheap.
- **Beast** (phases 1–3): reuses the **Werewolves mod werewolf model** at **2× scale**, new black-smoke texture, white-blue eyes, red vein overlay. No new model needed for a first version.
- **Spirit** (phase 4): a small flying entity with particles. No model.
- **Verdict:** it works under approach A with scale and texture, but it is a **GeckoLib-later candidate**, like Klaus's hybrid form and Deucalion's Demon Wolf, because the face (feline, lipless) and the shadow-wrapping transformation are what make the Beast read as the Beast. Recommendation: build it with the scaled werewolf model for the plan 17 release, and queue a GeckoLib model with the **Demon Wolf** and Klaus's hybrid in approach C (it would be the third showpiece).
- **Mod boundaries:** it needs the Werewolves mod (model). With Werewolves off the boss is not registered. The Host and Spirit can exist, but the encounter is gated on it. Part switch: `bosses`, config `beast_of_gevaudan_enabled`.
- **Pike rite** goes in the Enchanted rites part (`circle_magic/rite` data, with the `part_enabled` condition, see `docs/parts/enchanted_tensura.md` on why every rite file needs it).

## 4. Open questions for the owner

1. **Skull mask / season 6.** The Beast has no skull mask and is a season 5 villain. Did you mean the Anuk-Ite (season 6, a different boss), or just the Dread Doctor masks? *Recommend:* treat the Beast as season 5, skip the mask, and give the Anuk-Ite its own research task.
2. **Can a player force the Beast early?** *Recommend:* yes, a late rite (a "frequency" rite using the Hearing Device plus a full moon), so players who have a pike ready are not stuck waiting for the 25 % roll. Otherwise only the natural spawn exists.
3. **Show "Host" innocence.** Mason survives in the show. *Recommend:* the Host is **protected**: killing it (before phase 3) costs the encounter for that full moon, and it never counts as a player kill. Alternative: let it die and respawn once.
4. **Failure.** If the Spirit escapes, should the Beast return? *Recommend:* yes, once, next full moon at 50 % health and a new Host, so a failed fight is not final but cannot be farmed.
5. **Pike blood.** The show uses Valet blood, and Scott (no relation) wields it. *Recommend:* any performer's own blood, no hunter requirement; hunters just know the recipe first.
6. **Banshee exposure.** There is no banshee in the mod. *Recommend:* the "25 % inside a mountain ash line" trigger, with a fallback so the fight is not impossible without ash.
7. **Roar and Alphas.** *Recommend:* Alphas and True Alphas are immune to the fear, Omegas and Betas are not, so ranks matter.
8. **Reward.** *Recommend:* the Mantle of the Beast (shadow aura), no spark, no Pike drop. Alternative: a "Damnatio Memoriae" rite that hides your name from hunters (would overlap with Kol's Cloaking rite, so I avoided it).
9. **Numbers.** 1350 HP and the spawn chance are placeholders until the Alpha and True Alpha melee values are looked up in `docs/balance.md`. *Recommend:* tune after the first two-player run.
