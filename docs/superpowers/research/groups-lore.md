# Supernatural groups lore: covens, clans, packs

Research only, for SupernaturalBloodlines. Group type comes from the leader (werewolf-like = pack, vampire-like = clan, witch-like = coven).
Source tags: **[show]** = TVD / The Originals / Legacies / Teen Wolf. **[film]** = Underworld. **[folklore]** = real-world legend.

## 1. COVENS (witch-led groups)

### 1.1 Leadership and succession
- **[show]** A coven is "generally a group of twelve or more witches"; "covens seem to always have both a leader and followers". Tradition says 13: one leader plus twelve. Small, family-run covens defer to a leader or Elder. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
- **[show] New Orleans has nine covens.** Examples: French Quarter, Tremé, Ninth Ward, Garden District, Algiers, Gentilly. All follow one **Regent**. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
- **Regent: elected, not hereditary.** The Elders elect the Regent "by mutual agreement ... based on the candidate's leadership skills and needs of the community". The Regent is the "voice and the will of the Ancestors". https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Regent
  - Josephine LaRue was Regent until Dahlia killed her. The Elders wanted Vincent Griffith, who refused and nominated Davina. Davina won the post with a speech at Lafayette Cemetery ("Fire with Fire").
  - The Ancestors can reject a candidate (they refused Van Nguyen at first, then changed their minds). https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Regent
  - The community can strip the title. Attempting to kill the Regent is punishable by hanging. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Regent
- **Elder: appointed by other Elders.** If no Elders are left, a witch can **consecrate the remains of a powerful dead witch** and absorb that power. Precedent: after a 1742 massacre of the Elders, the rule became "whoever consecrated the most powerful dead witch in the community became an Elder". Sophie did this with Esther. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Elders
  - Elders are elected by the existing pool of Elders. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Elders
- **Harvest Witch: chosen by the existing French Quarter Elders.** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Elders
- **Gemini Coven: leadership by the Merge.** Twins reach 22; during a celestial event the stronger twin absorbs the weaker twin's magic and traits and becomes leader. The weaker twin dies. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Gemini_Coven , https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
  - The coven needs a Merge regularly so a new leader exists before the old one dies.
  - Coven-wide spells (the Prison Worlds) are bound to the leader's life. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Gemini_Coven
  - Joshua Parker used a Mercury-Venus conjunction when a full moon was not available. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Channeling
- **[show] Aja's Coven:** twelve witches, led by Aja. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
- **[show] The Travelers:** a very large coven led by Markos, who perform magic in groups because their curse blocks earth channeling. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Channeling

- Elders of each coven meet at Lafayette Cemetery with the Regent. This is the political body that sets policy and deals with other factions. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven

- **[show] Each New Orleans coven also keeps its own craft.** Examples: Sacrificial Magic, Representational Magic, Voodoo, Hoodoo, Necromancy. The Regent can draw on all of them. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven

### 1.3 What the group gives its members
- **Family, support and protection.** "A coven acts as a large organization or an extended family, offering support and protection to its members", with strict rules. **[show]** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
- **Ancestral Magic.** Living witches draw on the dead witches who were consecrated in the city's soil. The power is tied to the burial ground. Leaving the city cuts you off. **[show]** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancestral_Magic
  - It is "limited to the area where the witch's ancestors were buried". "Many ancestral witches are committed to their families and their coven." (same page)
  - Consecration: when a witch dies, the coven performs a ritual so her spirit joins the Ancestors and her magic feeds the pool. The living descendants keep drawing on it. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancestral_Magic
  - Sophie: "This cemetery is filled with the remains of our witch ancestors. Without access to them, we're powerless." (same page)
- **Regent powers.** One-time access to the full power of all Ancestors, plus access to the knowledge of every coven's craft ("Knowledge Tapping"). The Regent's reach is not limited to the city. **[show]** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Regent
- **Gemini connective magic.** The leader link "benefits the coven by making them all stronger". The price is that everyone's life is bound to the leader's. **[show]** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
- **Shared spell sets.** Gemini: cloaking spells, Prison Worlds, privacy spell. **[show]** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Gemini_Coven
- **Group rites and feasts.** *Fete des Benedictions* (community gives gifts to witches in exchange for blessings) and *Fete de Cadeau* (gifts to the Ancestors for magical favours). **[show]** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancestral_Magic , https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Regent
- **Sanctuary and borders.** Each coven keeps its own district. The coven also runs the Fauline asylum for witches who went mad. **[show]** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven

### 1.4 Combining and channeling power (owner's focus)
Source of truth for all items here: https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Channeling and https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Linking (**[show]**).

1. **Channeling** is a basic witch power: draw extra power from Nature, the elements, celestial events, other witches or supernatural beings. Luka: "Have you ever channeled another witch before? ... We can double our strength."
   - Witches channel other witches "by forming any connection, such as ... possession or willingly creating a link".
   - Bonnie + Luka (weather, opening a tomb). Bonnie + Kai (transporting more people than one witch could). Davina + Kol (disabling the White Oak Stake).
2. **Linking** ties two or more things together so sensation and injury are shared. "In the most extreme case, when used by a coven to share power, it comes at the cost of also sharing their life-force, such that if one dies, they all die" (Aja's Coven, Gemini).
   - **Aja's Coven (Because the Night):** the eleven others linked to Aja "so they could exponentially amplify her power". Caroline stabbed Aja and all twelve died.
   - Unlinking needs the same ingredients (blood of each linked party). Eva's link to eight witches was broken by Rebekah and Davina.
3. **Celestial events** are the standard "coven battery": sun, full moon, eclipses, comets, meteor showers, conjunctions, auroras, supernova. The rarer the event, the more power. The Gemini Coven used a lunar eclipse and an aurora to build the Prison Worlds. Davina used a "Nexus Vorti" (a miracle birth) to resurrect Mikael.
4. **Ancestral channeling.** The Harvest witches channel every consecrated witch buried in the city. Monique and Abigail linked hands and told the Mikaelsons they faced "the power of every single witch who had died in the history of New Orleans". Klaus broke the link by killing Abigail. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/French_Quarter_Coven
   - Elders can channel any single Ancestor (Genevieve with Celeste, Sophie with Esther). https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Elders
5. **The Harvest** (group rite, once per 300 years) https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Harvest
   - Four young witches are sacrificed in turn; each death passes her power to the next girl. Each girl stands for an element: Monique earth, Abigail air, Cassie water, Davina fire.
   - Only Elders can complete it. After the sacrifices, the Elders perform the Reaping and resurrect the four.
   - If the ritual is not completed within about a year, the coven first suffers disasters in order (earthquakes, wind, rain, fire) and then slowly loses ancestral magic. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancestral_Magic
   - Ceremony lines: Choosing: "To be reborn, we must sacrifice. Do you have faith in the Harvest?" Reaping: "Do you believe in the harvest? ... We call upon our elders to resurrect their chosen ones."
   - Davina, left alive, held all four girls' power and it tore her apart (earth shaking, storms, fire).
   - Esther's descendants (Hayley, the Mikaelsons) were needed to channel Esther's power to Sophie, who became an Elder by consecrating Esther.
6. **Rite of Nines** (dark group rite): link and sacrifice one witch from each of the nine covens; Eva Sinclair would have become "more powerful than any Elder or Harvest girl". The victims stayed alive and cloaked while linked. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Linking , https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Channeling
7. **Expression** (forbidden, dark magic). https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Expression
   - Drawn from massacres of twelve. The "Expression Triangle" needs 36 deaths (12 humans, 12 hybrids, 12 witches) and is strong enough to lower the Veil.
   - Shane: neutral power, "not monitored by Nature", used "however a witch sees fit". Sheila: "the manifestation of your will".
   - Costs: "can take a life of its own if it isn't properly controlled"; Caitlin Shane "died from overuse of Expression"; Bonnie died combining Spirit, Dark and Expression magic.
   - Aja's coven needed twelve linked witches just to "cleanse" one Expression user.
8. **Sacrifice, talismans, hotspots, doppelganger blood** are extra "ingredients" that substitute for a full moon: Qetsiyah's calcified blood, Papa Tunde's blade, magic hotspots (places where witches died violently), dark objects. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Channeling
9. **Traveler magic:** the curse made them unable to channel the earth, so their craft "heavily focuses on collective magic and performing of spells as a group". https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Channeling

**Costs and risks (all [show], same Channeling/Linking pages unless noted)**
- Hard cap per witch. Kol: "a witch can only ever channel so much power. After that, things start to get ugly ... they'll literally start to disintegrate from the inside."
- Donors weaken: Luka "felt weaker and fell on the floor", Bonnie got a nosebleed. Overuse causes nosebleeds, unconsciousness, death.
- Shared life-force: leader or link dies, everyone dies. The coven becomes a single point of failure.
- Source scarcity: celestial events, doppelganger blood, hotspots, are not always available.
- Spirits can cut off an abuser (they cut Bonnie off when she resurrected Jeremy).
- Ancestral dependence: if the Ancestors are released or the Harvest fails, the coven loses access. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancestral_Magic
- Over-concentration of power in one vessel (Davina after the interrupted Harvest). https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Harvest

### 1.5 What ends a coven, or exiles / punishes a member
- **Shunning (New Orleans).** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Shunning
  - Crimes: practicing dark magic, killing a fellow witch.
  - Rite: accused is brought before the Elders at Lafayette Cemetery; the Elders form two rows and each turns their back as the witch is marched out. Title and ancestral link are revoked. "To the New Orleans witches, it will be as though you were never born."
  - A shunned witch can still use magic but is weaker (Vincent).
  - Davina was shunned for the Kara Nguyen murder.
- Conspiracy against superiors: hanging. Questioning the Ancestors: death by magical bleeding. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
- **Fauline Asylum:** locked up and sedated for madness or forbidden magic. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven
- **Banishment to a Prison World:** the Gemini Coven banished Kai in a time-loop world. Jo bought her release from the coven by sealing Kai away. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Gemini_Coven
- **Destruction of a coven:** Gemini died when Kai (leader) died; Aja's coven died with Aja; the Regent role ended when the Ancestors were released (dissolved). https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Regent
- Other: the Elders can fall to massacre, leaving the coven unable to perform the Harvest (1742, and again in the Originals pilot).

## 2. CLANS (vampire-led groups)

### 2.1 Show: leadership and succession
- **The Strix (Elijah's sect).** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Strix **[show]**
  - Oldest vampire society, founded in the 12th century by Elijah with his protege Aya. Until Marcel, membership was only Elijah's sireline.
  - Leadership passed by default and by force: Elijah fled Mikael, Tristan de Martel took over, Tristan was imprisoned (Serratura), Aya led briefly, Marcel won.
  - **The Charter** is the sacred bylaws. Three clauses matter:
    - **Ludum Regale (Game of Kings):** any member can challenge the leader to a contest of strength *or* cunning; the leader must accept; whoever holds the Charter at midnight leads. Marcel won by hiding with locals who would not invite the Strix in. Elijah had arranged it so the Strix would accept an outsider.
    - **Sovereignty of the Founding Father:** "In the absence of a worthy leader ... complete dominion shall be restored to Elijah Mikaelson".
    - **Aliquid sub sole ("anything"):** only the leader can invoke it; the whole order then pledges to fulfil one command. Marcel is the only known leader to use it.
  - Recruitment: a card that shows a location when covered in blood; a trial (Marcel had to find who took his daylight ring before sunrise and kill them, or die). The trial was also a pretext to kill a plotting member.
- **Marcel's Army / the Quarter vampires.** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Marcel%27s_Army **[show]**
  - Marcel leads (Elijah later co-leads; Diego led the remnants after Marcel's exile). Loyalty and respect, not just fear: the army "preferr[ed] Marcel's rule which was built on respect and loyalty".
  - Marcel: "If your blood relations let you down, you make your own."
- **Sirelines.** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Sirelines **[show]**
  - A sireline is the chain of who sired whom. Esther's immortality spell added a link: if the originator is permanently killed, **everyone in the bloodline dies within hours**. "Each of the sirelines were at war" (Lucien). Elijah's death killed the Strix.
  - A Sireline Unlinking Spell exists (Davina and the Sisters unlinked Klaus; Elijah's stayed intact).
- **Sire bond.** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Sire_Bond **[show]**
  - Sired hybrids obey their sire; vampires sired by someone they loved as humans are bound too. "The bond affects how you act, not how you feel."
  - Breaking it: hybrids by turning ~100 times, vampires by turning off humanity or the sire releasing them.

### 2.2 Show: ranks and roles
- **Marcel's Army:** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Marcel%27s_Army **[show]**
  - **Daywalkers:** inner circle, hold a daylight ring (made by Davina), authority over Nightwalkers, guard secrets.
  - **Nightwalkers:** newly turned, the bulk of the army, used as infantry and show of force. They earn a ring by proving worth.
  - Later, Marcel stopped distinguishing; close lieutenants (Gia, Josh) gained a say.
- **Strix:** leader, second-in-command (Aya), inner circle, soldiers, a personal witch coven (the **Sisters**, outcast "lost souls" witches who give spells and predictions), and affiliates. Members are chosen for talent: artists, politicians, warriors. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Strix **[show]**

## 3. PACKS (werewolf-led groups)

### 3.1 Show (Teen Wolf)
Source for this block: https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/Teen_Wolf_Pack , https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/Werewolf , https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/Alpha_Werewolf , https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/The_Alpha_Pack (**[show]**).
- A pack is "one alpha and a group of Betas". The Alpha "leads and makes all decisions". Packs may include humans and other creatures (Scott's pack had a human, a banshee, a druid, a hunter, a werewolf).
- **Pack bonus:** werewolves "are better able to access their abilities with the support of a pack". In Season 5 ("A Credible Threat") Scott becomes physically stronger when his pack is back together.
- **Membership is voluntary; there is no joining ritual** (the wiki says so). Staying out leaves a wolf without allies: the **Omega** (lone wolf). "Omega of the pack" is also used as an insult for the lowest rank.
- **Becoming Alpha:** kill an Alpha; or **True Alpha** (strength of character, no killing, cannot be taken by normal means). Alphas create betas by bite or scratch; they have "limited mental control" over betas they create; Alpha eyes glow red; a beta who kills an innocent gets blue eyes.
- **Cost:** an Alpha who heals the ill by pain transference gives up part of their "spark" and can lose Alpha status (Derek for Cora).
- **Alpha Pack (Deucalion):** a pack of only Alphas. They claim to be democratic; Deucalion leads "through manipulation (Kali) and intimidation (the twins)". An Alpha who kills his own beta gains their power; Deucalion also wanted each member to kill their **Druid Emissary**. Most died or lost Alpha status. This is the "dark pack" mirror of Scott's pack.
- **Wolf Moon** ritual: "families/packs get together for a rite of passage" (Search for a Cure).
- Weaknesses useful for pack events: lunar eclipse, mountain ash, wolfsbane, Hecatolite.

### 3.2 Show (TVD / The Originals)
- **The Crescent Wolf Pack (New Orleans).** https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Crescent_Wolf_Pack **[show]**
  - Oldest pack in the setting (one of the "seven original packs"). Led by an Alpha. Royal families (Kenner, Labonair). Leaders: Lana, Oliver, Jackson, Hayley.
  - Succession by family and by **arranged marriage** to unite the pack (Hayley and Jackson). Oliver sentenced to death for treachery; Jackson exiled to the Bayou for refusing the Guerrera moonlight ring.
  - **Crescent Curse:** human only on the full moon. Cast by Celeste (possessing a Deveraux), lifted by Davina with the Ancestors' help. **Witch ties:** witches made the **moonlight rings** (Genevieve, with Klaus's blood as power source) that let wolves resist the moon. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/French_Quarter_Coven
  - **Unification Ceremony:** Hayley shared her hybrid ability to control her transformation with the whole pack, turning them into **evolved werewolves**. This is the best pack rite for a mod: the Alpha gives something to every member.
  - Crescent birthmark marks the bloodline.
- **Hybrid "packs" of a sire:** Klaus's hybrids are bound by the sire bond; Tyler's pack of ten hybrids broke it. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Sire_Bond **[show]**

## 4. FILM: Underworld (Eastern/Nordic covens, Elders, Council, Death Dealers, Lycans)

Used as the clan source because the shows are thin on formal vampire government. Pages are from the Underworld fandom wiki, read via the MediaWiki API.

### 4.0 Lycan packs **[film]**
Source: https://underworld.fandom.com/wiki/Lycan
- "In all incarnations of the franchise, a group of Lycans is called a 'Pack,' or a 'Clan.' The Leader ... is called an 'Alpha.'" (wiki trivia)
- Lycans are the second breed of werewolf (13th century), descended from the first werewolf line (William Corvinus). **Lucian** is the first Lycan: born human-and-wolf to a captive werewolf mother, kept by Viktor as a pet, and rose to commander. Lycans were bred as a **slave working class**, fitted with **Moon Collars** (silver spikes on the inside) to stop the Change at full moon.
- The 15th-century uprising nearly destroyed the vampires, which started the Vampire-Lycan War and the Purges. Marius later led the Lycan army (Marius's Army). **Evolved Lycans** came from injecting the Corvinus Strain.
- Lycans learn control with age: young ones change at the full moon, old ones at will ("channel their rage").
- Lycans can share memories through a bite. Silver is their only real weakness (silver nitrate in the blood is deadly).
- Group-feel: pack as a slave-and-rebel force with one charismatic leader (Lucian, Marius), versus vampires' hierarchical, "civilised" covens.

### 4.1 Vampire covens (clans) **[film]**
Source: https://underworld.fandom.com/wiki/Covens , https://underworld.fandom.com/wiki/Vampire_Elder , https://underworld.fandom.com/wiki/Vampire_Council
- A coven is a community of vampires who "gather together for ceremonies, rituals, and/or celebrations". Founded by the Elders (5th century and later). Examples: Old World, New World, Eastern, Nordic, Budapest, Paris.
- **Rank ladder:**
  1. **Elders (Grand Elders):** highest power; elect their advisers and Regents; can sentence members to death for treason (Viktor condemned his daughter Sonja for loving a Lycan); can found new covens; their descendants are raised to become Elders.
  2. **Council:** twelve seats in the Old World Coven; advises the Elders, judges breakers of coven law, **rules the coven when no Elder or Regent is present, and is the only body that can elect new Elders when all are dead.**
  3. **Regents / coven leaders:** chosen by the Elders to run a coven in their absence. If a Regent dies, an Elder or the Council picks a successor; if neither can, **the coven chooses by popular vote.**
  4. **Heads of House:** run the stronghold and make sure everyone does their job.
  5. **High-ranking members / dignitaries and envoys:** upper class, diplomats.
  6. **Aristocrats**, then **Death Dealers**, then **ordinary vampires** (servants, doctors, historians, notaries).
  - "Any Vampire can potentially rise through the ranks if they achieved enough respect and deeds." Low-borns rise after a full mortal lifespan or by war-hero reputation. Pure-born vampires start higher.
- **Death Dealers:** the coven's military and law enforcement. Trained to hunt Lycans, guard the coven, act as personal bodyguards to Regents/Elders. Anyone can volunteer. They come from Viktor's turned army (the first Death Dealers).
- **Elders' cycle, "The Chain":** one Elder rules for about a century while the other two sleep for two centuries in the crypt (Ordogház), so conflicting styles do not split the coven. The ruling Elder uses **Blood Sorting**: they pass organised memories through their blood to the Elder who wakes next. Only an Elder can wake another Elder well.
- **Selection of new Elders:** the Council promoted Selene, David (Amelia's son and rightful heir) and Lena after the old Elders died. Elder status can be inherited or elected.
- **Council politics:** the Eastern Coven Council issued a death warrant on Selene for killing Viktor, then gave clemency in exchange for training Death Dealers. Semira (Head of House) tried a coup. David's claim was proven by viewing Amelia's blood memories, and the Council and the Death Dealers sided with him.
- **Nordic Coven:** the "Var Dohr" sacred ritual (a cocooning rite) enhanced Selene and Lena (Wiki: Vampire Elder, "Var Dohr Sacred Ritual").
- **Ceremonies and parties:** covens hold soirees and ceremonies. Elders and the Council travel between covens; dignitaries arrive first to prepare.
- **Origin of bloodlines:** Marcus Corvinus (first vampire, bitten by Alexander Corvinus's line) sired Viktor and Amelia. Elders lied that the species' immortality was tied to all three Elders' bloodlines. Evolved vampires (Selene's line) are stronger and sun-immune.
- **Nordic Coven (Var Dohr)** **[film]** https://underworld.fandom.com/wiki/Nordic_Coven : founded in the 6th century by Amelia and Vidar. It answers to no Council, lives "away from the politics and the wars", and many believe it is a legend. Monastic, medieval, fortress on a frozen cliff (bell alarm at the gate, courtyard, armory, gathering room, **undercroft with pools where members are "cocooned"** for the Sacred Ritual). Its ritual raised Selene and Lena to a higher form. Amelia left her blood memories there with Vidar to prove David's heritage. It never hunted or enslaved Lycans (comic).
- **Eastern Coven** **[film]**: the modern stronghold with a Council of five, Death Dealer recruits, Head of House Semira (coup). Source: https://underworld.fandom.com/wiki/Covens
- **What ends it:** in the films, covens are destroyed by Lycan war and by the Purges; leaders are killed; Elders sleep. Treason is death. Marcus and the Elders' deaths left vampires leaderless for over a decade until the Council elected new Elders.

## 5. FOLKLORE

### 5.1 Coven folklore **[folklore]**
- **The word and the "13".** "Coven" comes from Anglo-Norman *covent* / Latin *conventum*. It was little used in English until 1921, when Margaret Murray promoted the idea that all European witches met in groups of thirteen. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coven
- **Modern Wicca practice** (same page): a coven gathers for rituals such as **Drawing Down the Moon** and the Sabbats; its meeting place is the *covenstead*. Thirteen is "ideal" to some, but any group of at least three is a coven; a pair is a "working couple". Many hold that a coven larger than thirteen is unwieldy.
  - **Splitting:** a coven that grows too large may "hive"; a newly made High Priest or Priestess may leave to found their own.
  - **Leadership:** usually jointly led by a High Priestess and a High Priest; newer groups sometimes run as democracies with rotating leadership.
  - The page's rite list names "Drawing down the Moon", "Cone of power", "Magic circle", "Great rite", "Spiral dance", "Esbat" and "Initiation". These are names only; the page does not describe the mechanics.
- **Benandanti ("good walkers").** Friuli, Italy, 16th-17th century. People born with a caul claimed their spirits left their bodies on certain nights to fight malevolent sorcerers (*malandanti*) with sorghum sticks, "to ensure good crops for the season to come". They were tried by the Inquisition between 1575 and 1675. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benandanti
  - Fits a coven **night-watch rite** that protects the community's crops and punishes outsiders.
- **Witch-hunt / persecution** is the real-world backdrop for the show's Salem link (Bennetts "come from Salem") and for the massacres that wipe out coven Elders. https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Coven (show) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coven (folklore).

### 5.2 Clan folklore (vampires) **[folklore]**
- **Strigoi (Romanian).** Troubled spirits risen from the grave; they can transform into a beast, become invisible, and "gain vitality from the blood of their victims". The word comes from Latin *strix* (screech-owl), the same source as the show's **Strix**. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strigoi
  - **Strigoi viu (living)** vs **strigoi mort (dead)**; "moroi" is a related term. Marks: seventh child of the same sex in a family, death before baptism, suicide, death under a witch's curse. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strigoi , https://legendarymythics.com/vampires/strigoi
  - The living strigoi's spirit leaves the body at night to fight other strigoi (or torment neighbours). https://complexdracula.ro/dracula/strigoi-vrykolakas-and-upir-comparative-mythology-of-eastern-european-vampires
- **Family-first predation.** The revenant goes first to its own family. Murgoci (via Kutztown): if the vampire is not stopped it works through its relations, then the village, then travels abroad, marries, and "the children, after they die, all become vampires and eat the relations of their mother". https://research.library.kutztown.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1016&context=dracula-studies
  - This is the folklore seed of a **bloodline / sire tree**.
- **Upir / vampir (Slavic).** Upir appears in an 11th-century Old Russian manuscript (1047). Slavic names include vorkudlak, obour, upir. https://legendarymythics.com/vampires/slavic-vampire-folklore , https://research.library.kutztown.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1016&context=dracula-studies
- **Lilith.** Jewish folklore: first wife of Adam who left him; became a blood-sucking demon, consort of the demon king, bearing "one hundred" demon children a day (the *lilim*). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilith , https://blog.nli.org.il/en/the_vampire_lilith
- **Cain as first vampire.** A modern literary theory, not ancient myth: Cain meets Lilith, who holds an "Awakening Ceremony" (she bleeds into a bowl, he drinks); three angels then curse him with fire weakness, sun weakness and blood-thirst; he becomes the "Dark Father" in Ubar, and his son Enoch asks to be turned. https://authorlyngibson.wordpress.com/2013/11/16/the-cain-vampire-origination-theory/ (self-published essay; use as flavour, not canon).
- **Sire and blood hierarchy.** The first-to-sire chain (Cain -> Enoch; Corvinus -> Viktor -> Death Dealers; Original -> sireline) is the common shape across all three sources.

### 5.3 Wolf pack folklore **[folklore]**
- **Ulfhednar (Norse wolf-skin warriors).** "Frenzy warriors wearing the skins of wolves", the "elite following" of King Harald Fairhair, said to wear a wolf pelt over mail; sagas present them as a group of berserkers; sometimes called Odin's special warriors. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulfhe%C3%B0nar
  - Haraldskvaedi: "Wolf-skinned they are called ... They form a closed group." https://www.skaldic.org (Haraldskvaedi, st. 21) and https://thethegns.blogspot.com/2012/03/berserkers-1-overview.html
  - Berserkers were used as shock troops and bodyguards; a berserker was weakest after the fury passed. https://www.historyextra.com/period/viking/the-truth-about-viking-berserkers
  - Sworn to the cult of Odin (same Thegns of Mercia page).
- **Loup-garou (French) and rougarou (Louisiana).** The curse lasts about 101 days; it is broken when someone draws blood from the transformed person, and the secret must be kept or the revealer is cursed. https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/loup-garou , https://64parishes.org/entry/rougarou
  - Folklore tie to morality: breaking Lent or missing Easter confession. Rougarou does not follow the full moon in the old tales (Skeptoid). http://skeptoid.com/episodes/1042
  - Cajun lore: werewolves "gather and hold big parties, dancing and howling around the fire, before they go out on a hunt"; thirteen small objects by the door confuse a rougarou that cannot count past twelve. https://www.theadvocate.com/acadiana/news/curious-louisiana-where-exactly-did-the-rougarou-come-from/article_1fcf8b2a-71ea-11ee-ac06-97f119e176ba.html , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rougarou
  - The New Orleans setting makes this the obvious local tie to the Crescent Pack.
- **Benandanti** are also a night-fighting company, a "guardian band" lore for packs (see 5.1).

## 8. BROTHERHOOD (hunter-led groups), added 2026-11-23 for plan 20d

Read through the MediaWiki API like the rest: https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Brotherhood_of_the_Five , https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter%27s_Mark , https://vampirediaries.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter%27s_Curse , https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/The_Argent_Family , https://teenwolf.fandom.com/wiki/Hunters . The shipped hunter rules are in `SPECIES-RULES.md` R80-R84 and R173.

### 8.1 The Brotherhood of the Five **[show]**
- Founded in 1110 AD: a dying witch (a descendant of Qetsiyah) binds five warriors, led by **Alexander**, to one goal; the spell gives peak human strength, an urge to kill vampires, and the **Hunter's Mark**. Their real task is to find, cure and kill the first immortal, Silas. They carry swords engraved with the group's symbol; the tattoo is read with them.
- At any time there are **five** hunters and **five** marks. When one dies, the mark appears on a **Potential** (only a hunter or Potential can see an incomplete mark); the Potential is **awakened by their first vampire kill**. Humans and the awakened are otherwise ordinary, so many hunters were "mostly unaware of one another".
- The original five were killed by Klaus in 1114 (the white-oak daggers did not work on a hybrid). Replacing all five took 52 years, 4 months, 9 days.
- Tools: swords of the Five, white oak ash daggers, vervain (darts, gloves, ropes, grenades), wooden bullets, werewolf venom, traps. Members work alone or in small groups; Galen Vaughn knows the true mission, Connor and Jeremy did not at first.

### 8.2 The Hunter's Mark **[show]**
- A tattoo from the back of the right hand up the arm and across the chest. It is a **map** (to Silas' tomb and the Cure), a **story**, a coded **message** (read with the sword's cryptex), an **empowerment** (strength, the urge to hunt, **immunity to compulsion**) and a **spell** (only a Qetsiyah descendant can use it to open the tomb).
- It **grows with every vampire kill** (pain, a stronger urge); it is a record of how many were killed. It shows the **Grand Sigil** first, then Amara and Qetsiyah, a pentagram in a ring, Silas, and five swords.
- **Completion:** Jeremy killed Kol with the white oak stake, which killed Kol's whole bloodline; the mark completed for him and **for every living hunter at once**, and became visible to everyone. The spell then took the mark from all of them; the bearers kept being hunters.
- An incomplete mark is invisible to non-hunters; losing a hunter resets the new bearer's progress.

### 8.3 The Hunter's Curse **[show]**
- A fail-safe in the same spell: **a hunter killed by what they hunt (a vampire, a hybrid, an immortal) curses the killer** with vivid hallucinations (the killer's own victims, first the hunter) that push them toward suicide, until a **Potential awakens** (the next first kill). Klaus endured it for 52 years, Elena until Jeremy awakened, Silas shook it off in minutes.
- **Humans are not cursed** (Julian compelled Rayna to kill her father so he would not be). The curse does not fire for a ghost hunter. It does not depend on the mark being visible.
- It does not stack: one curse, ended by the one awakening.

### 8.4 Kol and the hunters **[show]**
- Kol wanted Silas left buried. He killed the vampires Klaus made for Jeremy to kill, and **tried to cut off Jeremy's arm** so the mark could never be completed. Jeremy staked him instead. (Rebekah was the only Original who knew how to read the mark.)
- In the mod Kol already comes at night for a Hunter of the Five with enough prey kills (R173).

### 8.5 Mikael **[show]**
- Mikael is the Originals' hunter-father who slaughters vampires, the Strix and Marcel's army (section 2.4). In the mod he hunts a high-rung vampire player at night (bosses design).

### 8.6 Teen Wolf: the Argents and the Code **[show]**
- The **Argent family** has hunted for 400+ years ("powerful, wealthy, aristocratic"); the matriarch directs the local hunters; **males fight, females lead** (Chris explains). Allison trains with bow and blade; **silver bullets are forged as a symbol of finishing training**; the family keeps a **Bestiary** of every creature met.
- **The Hunters' Code: "Nous chassons ceux qui nous chassent" ("We hunt those who hunt us").** Hunt only werewolves that **harm humans, with proof**; **do not kill children / young wolves; do not kill humans.** Kate breaks it (the Hale fire); Gerard says the Code is dropped; the Calaveras enforce it strictly. There is also an honour code: a hunter who is **bitten takes their own life** (Victoria, Alexander).
- After Allison, Chris and Allison adopt a new code: **"We protect those who cannot protect themselves."** The hunters have contacts worldwide (Mexico, London, Brazil); a rogue group that kills for profit is denounced.
