# Plan 17: the bosses

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan phase by phase. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Nine once-per-world show bosses under their real names, each with its own encounter and one unique reward: Mikael, Klaus, Elijah, Kol, Rebekah, Finn, Deucalion, Peter Hale, and the Beast of Gévaudan (with its Host, Spirit, pike and rite). The six vampire Originals are the NPC roots of the bloodlines built in PR #34; killing one with white oak triggers line death. Human forms are player-model NPCs with the finished skins; creature forms are GeckoLib models.

**Branch:** `feature/bosses` is not used. Each phase below is its own branch and PR, built in order (`feature/bosses-17a` and so on). This plan was written on `docs/plan-17` (no PR of its own) and ships with phase 17a (PR #45).

**Sources:** `specs/2026-11-16-bloodlines-bosses-design.md` (sections 2 to 4 and 7, "Reward details"), `specs/2026-11-17-beast-of-gevaudan.md` (with the owner answers: season 5, no mask, Valet Pike by a witch's rite with hunter or advanced vampire/werewolf blood), `specs/2026-11-17-hub-information-architecture.md` (rows for plan 17, section 3.7 knowledge table), `specs/SPECIES-RULES.md` (R32, R33, R120 to R127, R143 to R149), `plans/2026-11-16-original-lines.md` (what it left for this plan: `NpcOriginal`, `ORIGINAL_ROOT`, `LineDeath.run`, `LineWarning`, `line_exempt`, `markDead`), `plans/2026-11-18-npc-packs.md` (conventions, the matrix, `NpcPackSpawner`), and the art READMEs (`art/bosses`, `art/items`, `art/items/RING_RENDER.md`, `art/creatures/*`).

## Decisions made while planning (reversible; each is cheap to flip)

1. **A new part, `bosses`** (`Part.BOSSES("bosses", ...BossesPart, List.of("vampirism", "enchanted", "geckolib"))`, `requiredParts = SPECIES`). Plan 15 rejected a new part for plain code in `species`; this plan is different: it adds the **first entity types, renderers, GeckoLib, a structure and a SavedData this mod owns**, and the hub IA already lists a `bosses` part switch (IA row 110). "Needs `SPECIES`" is the documented way `speciesTensura` imports `species` (checked: `speciestensura` imports `parts.species.*`), so it is not a new exception. Mixin budget: **bosses 0 of 3**, species stays 3 of 3.
2. **GeckoLib needs no new Gradle line.** `build.gradle` already has `compileOnly` and `localRuntime` for `geckolib-neoforge-1.21.1:4.9.3` (Tensura needs it), and **Enchanted declares GeckoLib `type="required"`, `[4.7,)`** in its `neoforge.mods.toml` (read from the 4.2.7 jar). Every world that has the `bosses` part's other required mod (Enchanted) therefore has GeckoLib, so making the part require `geckolib` costs nobody anything. Our `neoforge.mods.toml` gets a `geckolib` entry as `type="optional"`, `ordering="AFTER"`, range `[4.7,5)` (an out-of-range optional mod stops FML, so the range spans the series, as for the other linked mods), and `LinkedMods.TESTED` gets `geckolib 4.9`. Making it a `required` dependency would break the "every linked mod is optional" rule and the `-PnoLinkedMods` run for no gain.
3. **Our bosses are plain `PathfinderMob`s, not Vampirism entities** (no `IVampire`, not `VampirismEntity`). That avoids sun burning, Vampirism's bite and blood data, conversion and hunter-faction targeting, and `NpcLines`' random root for every Vampirism vampire. They are still added to `#supernaturalbloodlines:line_exempt` (data) as a second guard, and to `#supernaturalbloodlines:supernatural_mobs` so a mountain ash line stops them (the original-lines plan requires Original boss entities to stop at mountain ash; `MountainAshLineBlock.blocks` reads that tag, checked).
4. **One entity type per boss body, with a form.** Klaus, Deucalion and Peter are one entity each with a synced `FORM` (`HUMAN`, `SHIFTING`, `BEAST`) and a small `ShiftRenderer` that delegates to the humanoid renderer or the `GeoEntityRenderer` by form, so the UUID, health, boss bar, `ORIGINAL_ROOT` and index entry never change when they shift (the alternative, two entity types swapped on shift as the Werewolves mod does for `HumanWerewolfEntity`, loses all of that). The hitbox follows the form (`refreshDimensions()` on form sync). **The Beast is different**: its Host is a separate protected person, so it is three entity types (`gevaudan_host`, `beast_of_gevaudan`, `gevaudan_spirit`).
5. **Once per world lives in a SavedData, `BossIndex`** (`boss -> state, entity uuid, last pos and dimension, return day, rewarded, appearances`), not in the entity. States: `UNSEEN`, `ACTIVE`, `OPEN` (an Original desiccated), `DEAD`, `LOST` (entity not found after its chunk loaded; the director may bring the same boss back, and `rewarded` stays set). The director is one `LevelTickEvent.Post` hook on the overworld every `boss_check_seconds`, asking a pure `SpawnRules` with facts. No vanilla spawn rules, no biome modifier.
6. **No new AI injection problem here**: plan 15 avoided goals because the mobs were foreign. These entities are ours, so they use `registerGoals` with vanilla goals plus a few of our own (`BossLeapGoal`, `NightHuntGoal`, `FleeFromAshGoal`).
7. **Rewards and the line**: a boss gives its reward once (`BossIndex.rewarded`). When it is paid is open question 1; the default plan pays on the **first defeat** (desiccation or death), so nobody must trigger a line death to get Rebekah's True Cure.
8. **A weak-spark-style grant, not a spark item.** Deucalion's and Peter's sparks go straight to the killer through a new `SparkHandler.grantFromBoss(killer, sparks)` that uses the same `PackRules.stealBy` ladder as `steal` (TAKES, PROTECTED, NEVER) and `sparksAfterSteal` with `max_stacked_sparks`. The `alpha_spark`, `true_alpha_spark` and `npc_alpha_spark_0..2` art becomes **hub icons only**, no registered item (open question 4).
9. **Rites over targeting.** The three new rites (Cloaking, Linking Spell, Valet Pike, plus the frequency rite) read what is in the circle with `getEntitiesOfClass(..., AABB(pos).inflate(RADIUS))`, the way `RequiresDesiccatedRiteFactory` already does (checked), so the unverified `RiteTargetingType.ENTITY` question of plan 15 (U1) is not needed.
10. **Rule numbers R150 to R169** are reserved in `SPECIES-RULES.md` for this plan (R149 is the last used).

## Common to every phase

- **TDD, rule tables first.** Each phase starts with a pure class in `core/boss/` and a JUnit class with one test per table row (the `PackRulesTest` style), then glue, then GameTests. Every test is checked to fail with its rule reverted; the PR lists the mutants.
- **UI first.** Every config key has an Admin row (`AdminCoverageTest`, `UiReferencesTest` fail otherwise). Every `/supernaturalbloodlines boss ...` form has an Admin button (`AdminCommandsTest`, OP-only). Every player-facing feature has a hub path in all three themes, lang keys pass `HubLangCoverageTest`. Hidden safe shots only (`runSafeShots` under `focus-watch.ps1`), scale 2 and 4, saved in `docs/superpowers/design/bosses/`. **Never run a Minecraft client by hand.**
- **Server authority, old saves.** Every hub action is a server-validated payload. Every new codec field is `lenientOptionalFieldOf`. `BossIndex` skips a bad entry, not the file (the `PackIndex.load` style).
- **Part switch.** `-PpartsOff=bosses` must leave the game as it is without the part: no entity type, no item, no data (all data uses `supernaturalbloodlines:part_enabled` with `"part": "bosses"`, tags with `"required": false`), no hub row. `bosses` is added to the CI part list and the matrix.
- **Matrix.** Each PR runs the 10 runs of plan 18 plus `-PpartsOff=bosses` (CI also has `celestials`): `build test`, full GameTests, `-PnoWerewolves -PnoCurios`, `-PnoLinkedMods`, `-PpartsOff` for species, artifacts, speciesTensura, supernatural, supernaturalTensura, hud, bosses. Werewolves-only bosses' GameTests register only when Werewolves is loaded.
- **Docs per PR.** `docs/parts/bosses.md` (new, from 17b), `docs/parts.md` row, `docs/dependencies.md` (GeckoLib: "required by the bosses part, which Enchanted already guarantees"), `docs/mixins.md` ("bosses 0 of 3, none added"), `SPECIES-RULES.md` (R150 to R169), `docs/balance.md`, wiki (new `Bosses` page from 17b, `Rites`, `Items-And-Blocks`, `Configuration`, `Commands`, `Known-Limits`), `CHANGELOG.md`.
- **No push to main.** Each PR waits for green CI and the review panel.
- **Shared test seams.** `BossDirector.useRoll(DoubleSupplier)` and `BossIndex` reset hooks (like `NpcLines.useRoll`), a `FakeBossSource` for the lunar mood, a `BossTestRig` that spawns a boss with fixed facts. Every test that unmarks a dead root restores it.

# Open questions for the owner (all 15 decided: the owner accepted every recommended answer)

1. **DECIDED (recommended answer).** **When is a boss reward paid?** On the **first defeat** (desiccation or death), or only on a white oak death? *Recommend first defeat* (`boss_reward_on = first_defeat`): a reward that needs white oak forces a line death on the whole server just to get Rebekah's True Cure, which is the anti-goal "a player dying to a line death with no warning". `death` stays as a config.
2. **DECIDED (recommended answer).** **Can white oak kill an NPC Original that is not open?** Today (R32) it kills a *player* Original instantly. *Recommend the NPC only dies while open* (desiccated or in the dying state), so a craftable stake does not one-shot a boss; config `npc_original_stake_needs_open` (default true).
3. **DECIDED (recommended answer).** **How long does a desiccated NPC Original stay down?** *Recommend 5 in-game days* (`original_return_days`), so a line has a real window to warn and act, and the blood vial drops at most once per window.
