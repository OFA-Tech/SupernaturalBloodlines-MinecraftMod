# Plan 20d: Brotherhood (hunting together, shared prey sight, the Code, the Curse for every brother, leadership by the mark): Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give the brotherhood type of plan 20a its perks. A group whose leader is a **hunter** (Vampirism hunter faction, Hunter of the Five, Potential) **or a plain human** is a brotherhood. Four things, all owner-approved: (1) **Hunting together:** every other brother who fits and stands within 24 blocks gives +3% attack damage, up to +12%, and a brother with the prey sense (5 or more kills) **shares what they sense** with the brothers who lack it; **kills and mark progress are never shared**. (2) **The Code** ("We hunt those who hunt us") **punishes only**: a brother who kills an innocent loses 25% of the mark (at least 1 kill when they have any), is a **Code-breaker** for 24 real hours (no hunting-together bonus given or received, no shared sight, no lead), and a **third break within 7 days expels** them (barred from that brotherhood for 7 days). (3) **The Hunter's Curse reaches every brother:** a vampire (any prey killer, as R83) who kills any brother is cursed, the curse does not stack, and when the dead brother had no mark it **ends after 48 real hours or when a Potential awakens**. (4) **Leadership by the highest mark:** the moment an online brother's mark is **strictly higher** than the leader's, the lead moves to them at once; a tie keeps the leader. The cure-map tattoo on the Brotherhood page is **display only**. Every perk check is one question, `BrotherhoodScope.appliesTo(group is a brotherhood AND this member fits it: a hunter or a plain human)`, so plan 20e (groups of several types, each member getting only the perks of the types they fit) changes that helper and nothing else. No new Part, no new mixin, no new item, no new command.

**Architecture:** Pure rules in `core.group.brotherhood` (fit, scope, mark and its loss, lead-by-mark, the arms count, the sight table, the Code table and ledger, the curse end, the map regions, the view and its wire bounds). Three new rows in the existing `GroupIndex` (`marks`, `curses`, `code_breakers`; expulsion reuses the 20c `bans` rows by group id, so an expelled brother is refused by the same `GroupGate.exiled` check as an exiled clan member). The lead moves through `GroupLead.handOver` (the one path the absence rule, the coven election and the Game of Kings share). The hunter code changes in **three small places only** and never for a player who is in no brotherhood: `MarkTracker` gets a public kill feed (`onDeathResolved`, the same attribution and the same `LOW`, not-cancelled listener the mark already uses) and a public `isPrey`; `HunterInstinct.sense` gets a hook list next to its one `GlowPayload.send`; `HunterCurse` gets public single-player lifts and an `onLift` hook. Kol's warning is one direct call from `BossDirector.spawnKol` into `KolShadow` (the `bosses` Part already requires `species` and already imports its classes; the reverse direction is the isolated one, so no seam is needed). One new S2C payload (`BrotherhoodViewPayload`, **primitives only**) carries the viewer's own brotherhood card; every number is a config key with an Admin row; the page cards are drawn in the three themes.

**Tech Stack:** Java 21, NeoForge 21.1.265 (MC 1.21.1), Vampirism 1.10.13, Werewolves 2.0.3.4 (compile only, optional at run time), JUnit 5, the repo's GameTest runner.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-groups-design.md](../specs/2026-11-23-groups-design.md) (sections 2.1, 3, 8b, 9, 10, 11, 13 item 9, 14, 16.2; this plan corrects it where the owner's later scope or the code says otherwise, see "Spec corrections"). Base plans: [20a](2026-11-23-plan-20a-groups-base.md) and [20b](2026-11-24-plan-20b-covens.md) (merged), [20c](2026-11-24-plan-20c-clans.md) (build PR #106 on `feature/plan-20c`, **merges first**; this plan reads its code from there). Lore: [research/groups-lore.md](../research/groups-lore.md) section 8. Hunter rules: `SPECIES-RULES.md` R80-R84, R173.

Repo conventions that apply: one branch `feature/plan-20d-brotherhood`, one PR, a `CHANGELOG.md` entry with a "Rolling back" paragraph, `docs/parts/species.md` ticked, the rules copied into `docs/superpowers/specs/SPECIES-RULES.md` as **R290 to R299** (plan 21 uses R300-R329), **no show names in player text (R5)**, no narrating code comments, hidden hub shots per `docs/safe-screenshots.md`.

## Spec corrections (made in this PR, in the spec)

1. **3.1 (derivation):** a **plain human leader derives BROTHERHOOD**, not COVEN (owner scope, 2026-11-25). A hunter still derives it first; a leader who is a human **whose Tensura race is not human-like**, a witch, a siphoner who is not a vampire and a heretic still derive COVEN. **3.2 (founding) is unchanged:** BROTHERHOOD is founded by a hunter (`Fit.hunter`); `open_founding` lifts the gate. A plain human *leads* a brotherhood (as an heir, as a hunter who left the hunters, in an existing group, or with `open_founding`) and may join one (owner answer 1).
2. **R290 (join):** the spec's `prey_member` refusal and the `released_prey` sweep are **not built in 20d**: join rules belong to plan 20e. The same protection is the scope: a member who does not fit (a witch, a vampire, a werewolf, a hybrid) is a member with **no brotherhood perks**, gives none and gets none (R298). The keys `prey_member` and `released_prey` are not created.
3. **R292 and R293:** "each other brother who is a hunter" becomes "each other brother who **fits** (a hunter or a plain human)", and the sight reaches "every other brother who fits and lacks the sense" (owner: each member gets the perks of the types they fit; the lore seed H1 lists humans as members).
4. **R291:** "and at least a Potential" is dropped: a strictly higher mark than the leader's (0 or more) is 1 or more, which only a Hunter of the Five has, so the clause could never be false. A Code-breaker **cannot take or inherit** the lead; a leader who breaks the Code is not deposed by that alone (the mark loss usually does it).
5. **R294:** the strike counter is cleared when it expels; **a leader is never expelled** (owner answer 2: the third break gives them a fresh 24 h breaker day, clears the strikes, and the mark loss still applies).
6. **R295:** the 48-hour end **keeps running when `curse_extends_enabled` or `brotherhood_enabled` is turned off** (a switch that is off removes a feature, never strands a cursed player); a killer who already carries the curse (timed or not) is left alone, as R83.
7. **R297 and 10.1 (names):** the region labels are plain words (R5: no show names): the sigil, the twin marks, the ring and the star, the sleeper, the five swords, the whole map. The saying "We hunt those who hunt us" is a quotation, not a name, and is the Code card's one line.
8. **R296 and 10.1 ("a Kol warning toast"):** the warning is one chat line plus the action bar; there is no new toast system. The line names the target and never Kol or what he is.
9. **R298 and R299** carry dormancy and privacy (the numbers of 20c's R280 and R281); R298 "Mikael's call" is not built and takes no number.
10. **11 (wire):** the payload is `BrotherhoodViewPayload` (the 20b and 20c shape), not a block of `GroupViewPayload`; it carries counts and flags only; `LadderNet.PROTOCOL` "19"; no request kind.
11. **8b.2 R290 (labels):** the page says First Brother, Brother and Potential. The Potential word reaches the page through the existing rank string of the member row (`GroupViews`), computed on the server from `FiveRoster.isPotential`.
12. **13 item 9:** the four unverified hooks are read (see the table); Task 0 proves each in a GameTest.

## Owner answers, 2026-11-25

The owner accepted all six recommended picks. They are decided, not open:

1. **Only a hunter can found a brotherhood** (Task 4: Fit and canFound(BROTHERHOOD) unchanged). A plain human can **lead** one (as an heir, as a hunter who left the hunters, in an existing group) and **join** one; a plain human never takes the lead by mark (their mark is 0).
2. **The leader is never expelled** (Task 8): a third break clears the strikes and gives a fresh 24 h Code-breaker status; the 25% mark loss of that break still applies.
3. **Plain hunters, humans and Potentials have mark 0**; only a Hunter of the Five has kills (Tasks 2 and 5).
4. **A Code break is the spec table only** (Tasks 2 and 8): a villager, or a non-prey player who did not hit the killer in the last 10 seconds, including witches and siphoners who did not fight. No golems, traders or pets.
5. **Shared sight goes to hunters and plain humans** who lack the sense, within 48 blocks of the sensor, same dimension (Task 7).
6. **The type follows the leader and the data sleeps** (Tasks 4 and 5): a leader who becomes a vampire, a werewolf or a witch switches the group to the type that kind derives, members stay, brotherhood rows go dormant; a hunter who only leaves the faction is a plain human and **keeps** the brotherhood.

There are no open questions left in this plan.
