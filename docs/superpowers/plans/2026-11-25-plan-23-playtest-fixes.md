# Plan 23: Playtest fixes (race mapping, human-like set, skills and HUD, keys and menus, the worn book, rites, portraits): Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fix what the owner's playtest of 2026-11-25 found, as seven owner decisions (A to G). **A. Race mapping:** a Tensura race picked in the reincarnation menu (or reached later) gives the matching kind: `tensura:ghoul` is **our Ghoul in transition** (vampire faction level 1 plus the Ghoul flag, exactly like a finished Sanguinare), Tensura's own Ghoul to Vampire evolution keeps working; `tensura:direwolf`, the 12 Mysticism `*_spirit_wolf`, the fang, star, tempest, frost and scorch wolves, `divine_wolf` and the Beastfolk line (`beastfolk`, `beast_lord`, `spirit_beast`, `divine_beast`) give the **Werewolves faction with an Omega, born start and never a rank, Alpha or spark**; Ascension's five Bloodfiends give the vampire faction with level floors. It runs a tick after every non-refused `SET_RACE`, never lowers anything, does not re-map cures or admin edits, runs once at login for existing players who lack the kind (with a message) and has an Admin "sync race" button. **B. Skills and HUD:** Home's kind chip says what the server says; the Loadout page lists only slottable powers, greyed rows say why ("can't use now: ..."), a slot is cleared (and re-offered later) when its power stops being available, Bite is never listed or accepted for a non-biter, Vision needs its skill, Force-feed and Vision get real icons, and the "unknown" picture never shows for our own actions. **C. Keys and menus:** every compat key bind (Tensura, Skill Trainer, Horizons, Magic Growth, Ascension, Slime Throne, Great Sage, Infinity, Vampirism) is hidden from Controls **always** while our client is installed; menus move to a new hub page "Other mods", contextual actions go on the skill wheel, debug keys become OP toggles in Admin. **D. The Art of Witchcraft** can be worn in the charm slot (that book only) and is drawn at the hip. **E. Rites:** every refusal also goes to chat and names the missing need; the full moon, eclipse and Moonstone are explained where a player reads about a rite; one shared "is werewolf" rule; one night window. **F. Portraits:** Home, Profile and the pack ring's faces stop overflowing their round ring (one square frame everywhere). **G. Human-like races:** the set is read dynamically from Tensura and its addons, our own races are added on top, admins can add or remove races per world from the hub (OP only), and every place that asks "human-like" reads one shared helper.

**Architecture:** Pure rules in `core` (`core.race.RaceKindTable` the id-to-kind table and its switches, `core.race.MapRules` the "never lowers, never re-maps" decisions, `core.race.HumanLikeSet` the set algebra: dynamic default, config default, world additions and removals, `core.hud.LoadoutRules` the availability, clear and re-offer rules, `core.hud.KeyPlan` the key list and the Alt-modifier plan, `core.moon.MoonText` the facts the moon texts must state, `shared.hub.kit.PortraitGeometry`). The mapping listener lives in `vampirism_tensura` (the part that already owns the Tensura-race to Vampirism-faction sync) and reaches species only through one seam, `shared.species.RaceKindSeam`, installed by the species part (the `TurningFeed` pattern), whose implementation `parts.species.pack.RaceAwakening` is modelled on `GeneHandler.awakenHuman` and `GhoulGates`. A shared `shared.tensura.RaceSettled` runs an action **a tick after** a race change only if the race is still the one seen (the `ApexFallback` pattern: a later `SET_RACE` listener may still refuse). Human-like is one class, `shared.tensura.HumanLike`, installed by species_tensura (`TensuraHumanLikeCheck` becomes its Tensura source) and answered by a world-saved `HumanLikeData` plus config lists; `SpeciesEligibility`, `TurningHandler.isHumanLikeBlood`, `SpeciesContext`, `PotentialChooser`, the doppelganger rite and the 20d/20e group fit read it. The keys are covered by the existing `KeyCover`/`CoveredKeys`/`CoverRun` machinery with the policy changed (no world and no server condition), the Other-mods page is one hub page (`HubPage.OTHER_MODS`) whose buttons call small reflective openers (`shared.hub.OtherMods` seam, installed by the HUD part), and the contextual wheel actions are one more client-side entry source in `WheelList` next to `GreatSageProvider` and `InfinityCompat`. The worn book is a Curios/Accessories charm tag, a `shared.hub.WornBook` helper (moved out of `parts/artifacts/curios`) and a `BookRenderer` copied from `RingRenderers`. Rites get one `shared.rite.RiteTell` (chat plus action bar) and the 29 rite files that call `displayClientMessage` go through it. **No new Part, no new mixin, no new item, no new payload class** (unless Task 19 picks the payload route; then one protocol bump with a golden test).
