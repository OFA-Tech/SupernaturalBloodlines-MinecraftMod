# Plan 20e: Multi-type groups (one group, several types; who may join; perks by member; automatic release; every succession): Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let one group hold **several types at once**, as the owner decided on 2026-11-25. (1) **The types of a group come from all of the leader's kinds:** a hybrid or Original hybrid leads a **pack and clan**; a tribrid a **pack, clan and coven**; a heretic or a vampire-siphoner a **clan and coven**; a witch-werewolf a **pack and coven**; a plain human or a hunter a **brotherhood** (only a hunter can found one); a plain werewolf, vampire or witch still leads the one type they always did. (2) **Who may join:** a pack takes every kind (humans too), a clan takes vampire-like kinds, a coven takes witch-like kinds (witch, tribrid, heretic, siphoner), a brotherhood takes humans and hunters; in a mixed group **anyone allowed by any of its types** may join. (3) **Perks by member:** each member gets only the perks of the types they personally fit (only vampire-like members get a clan's shared feeding); every scope helper (`ClanScope.appliesTo`, `BrotherhoodScope.appliesTo`, the coven and pack checks) becomes "the group has type X **and** this member fits X". (4) **Automatic release:** a member who stops fitting **every** type of the group (turned, cured, or the leader's change dropped a type) is removed with a notice to them, no ban. (5) **Coven NPCs:** only witch-like Tensura subordinates of members, joining by themselves as pack NPCs do; no Enchanted or vanilla witch recruiting. (6) **Leadership:** every type's succession works (the Alpha duel, the Game of Kings, the Regent election, the absence heir); whichever moves the lead first wins, the other contests end with no winner, and the types are recomputed from the new leader. (7) **Hub icon:** cycles through the group's type icons every 2 seconds; with **Reduce motion** it shows the leader's main type only, and the tab's tooltip lists every type.

**Architecture:** Pure rules in `core.group` (`GroupTypeSet`, `GroupKinds`, the set derivation `GroupTypes.setOf`, `JoinRules`, `ReleaseRules`, `ChallengeRoute`, the set-aware `GroupSwitch`, `GroupSuccession.firstHeir`, `GroupIcons.frame`). The **main type** of a group stays the single type `GroupTypes.of` gives today: it keeps the existing `types` row of `GroupIndex`, is what `Groups.typeOf*` still answers, drives the icon under Reduce motion, and is why an old single-type save loads unchanged and a rolled-back jar re-derives the same type with no flip. The **set** is two new rows (`type_set`, `type_terms`) in the same `GroupIndex`; a missing row means "just the main type". Every perk and membership rule asks one of the scopes (`ClanScope`, `CovenScope` (new), `BrotherhoodScope` (20d), `PackScope` (new)), never `type == X` on its own; a source-scan test pins that. Joining is checked at `accept` (never at `invite`, so an invitation tells the leader nothing about the invited), release runs from the bloodline listener, login, set changes and the 200-tick sweep, and the lead still moves through `GroupLead.handOver` only. One changed S2C payload (`GroupViewPayload`: the type set, the viewer's own fit, a rank mask per member row), `LadderNet.PROTOCOL` to the integer after 20d's. The hub page gains a **type pill row** (client only) so a group with three types shows one type's cards at a time; a single-type group's page is pixel-identical to today's. No new Part, no new mixin, no new item, no new payload class.

**Tech Stack:** Java 21, NeoForge 21.1.x (MC 1.21.1), Vampirism 1.10.13, Werewolves 2.0.3.4 (compile only, optional at run time), Tensura (optional, `species_tensura`), JUnit 5, the repo's GameTest runner.

## Spec corrections (made in this PR, in the spec)

1. **3.1 (derivation):** a leader has a **set** of types, not one; the order "first match wins" now picks only the **main type** (icon, Reduce motion, saves). Table: hybrid and Original hybrid pack+clan; tribrid pack+clan+coven; heretic and vampire-siphoner clan+coven; witch-werewolf pack+coven; plain human and hunter brotherhood alone (A5 stays: a hunter-like leader's set is the brotherhood whatever else they are).
2. **3.2 (founding):** a leader may found when **any** type of their set allows it (`canFound` per type, OR); `open_founding` still lifts every gate. An existing group's leader keeps leading what they lead; only a pack-only set still needs an active Alpha.
3. **3.4 (a switch):** a "switch" is now a change of the **set**: what is lost goes dormant, what is gained starts (with a stamp per type), what stays is untouched (an open vote survives a pack gaining a clan). Nobody is kicked **for the switch**; release (R334) is a separate rule that removes only a member who fits none of the new set.
4. **4 R242 (join):** the gate is also **who may join** (R332), checked at `accept`, never at `invite`.
5. **4 R244 and R245:** the absence heir and the challenge take the set: the first type with an heir wins; the challenge keeps its three forms (unnamed: the clan's Game of Kings; a named leader: the pack's duel; a named Elder: the coven's call for a Regent), and `accept` and `decline` answer the older open request.
6. **8b.2 R290 (brotherhood join):** 20d did not build the spec's `prey_member` refusal and `released_prey` sweep; 20e builds the owner's rule instead: a brotherhood takes **humans and hunters** (not "anyone who is not prey": a witch or siphoner no longer joins), and a member who stops fitting is released by R334. 20d's BrotherhoodScope "a member who does not fit gets nothing" stays true for a legacy member.
7. **10 (hub):** the Group page gains a type pill row for a group with more than one type; the tab icon cycles (R337).
8. **11 (wire):** `GroupViewPayload` carries the set, the viewer's fit and a rank mask per row; `LadderNet.PROTOCOL` `"20"`.
9. **14 (risks):** two rows added (a release purge on upgrade; a mixed group's page overflow), see "Risks".

## Review Focus

Inputs the spec implies and no happy path covers, most likely first. Each has a test in the task named in brackets.

1. **The main type is in the set, always, and is today's answer.** A grid over every species and every flag combination: `setOf(l)` contains `of(l)`, and `of(l)` is unchanged. This is what makes an old save load with no flip and a rolled-back jar re-derive the same type. [Task 2 `theMainIsAlwaysInTheSet`, Task 3 `theTypesRowStaysTheMain`]
2. **A hybrid is clan-like now.** The old `ClanScope.fits` table said a hybrid did not fit a clan; the new one says it does, and a witch member of a tribrid's group still gets no clan feeding. [Tasks 2, 5]
3. **No oracle at the door.** The invite for a non-fitting kind is identical to any other; `accept` refuses with the shunned/exiled line; the leader hears nothing; the token is consumed; a leader who joins another group brings members, and one non-fitting member refuses the lot without saying who. [Task 6]
4. **Release is quiet and safe.** Only the released hear it; no ban is written (they may rejoin a group that takes them); the leader is never released; a pack never releases anyone (it takes every kind); an offline member is judged at login; a 30-second grace (in memory) stops a transient state from ejecting; the switch off keeps members as perk-less members. [Task 7]
5. **A set change must not void what stays.** A pack-and-clan group that gains a coven keeps its open clan contest; one that loses its clan ends the contest and keeps the Charter row dormant; a coven vote survives a clan being gained; per-type stamps decide. [Tasks 3, 4]
6. **Legacy groups are adopted silently.** A single-type group with no `type_set` row gets its derived set at the first refresh with **no notice** (nothing was lost); a **stale** row (rollback then upgrade) that does not contain the stored main type is ignored. [Tasks 3, 4]
7. **The upgrade purge.** An existing coven, clan or brotherhood that holds members the new rules would refuse (20c and 20d never refused anyone) loses them at the first sweep after upgrade, one notice each. The CHANGELOG says so first, and `release_unfit_members` (and `join_rules_enabled`) turn it off. [Tasks 7, 12]
8. **Two lead routes at once.** A duel and a Game of Kings (a pack-and-clan group), a Game of Kings and a Regent call (clan and coven), a duel and a Regent call (pack and coven) can all be open; the first to move the lead wins, the others end with no winner and no cooldown; the new leader's kinds give the new set, and the old leader becomes the next rank of **every** type they still fit. [Task 8]
9. **The challenge needs no new literal.** The unnamed form is the clan's; a pack's duel and a coven's call both take a name, and the name tells them apart (the leader: the duel; an Elder who is not the leader: the call for a Regent); `accept` and `decline` answer the **older** of two open requests. Each page's button sends its own type's command, so no button and no `AdminCommands` row is added. [Task 8]
10. **Only the right NPCs.** A witch-like Tensura subordinate of a coven member joins the coven; a clan or a brotherhood takes none (and releases what it wrongly bound before 20e); a pack still takes every subordinate; vanilla and Enchanted witches never; the cap is the pack's. [Task 9]
11. **The page never overflows.** A group with three types shows one type's cards at a time (pills); a single-type group's page is pixel-identical to before; the card rectangles hold in the three themes and the flat page at gui scale 2 and 4. [Task 11]
12. **Reduce motion and the cycle.** With it on, the icon is the main type's at every tick; with it off, 40 ticks per type, main first, wrapping; a viewer in no group sees the paw; the tooltip lists every type of the group and says nothing to a viewer in none. [Tasks 2, 11]
13. **The wire is bounded.** A mask of 0, above 15, a main not in the mask, or a fit outside the mask fails that one packet cleanly; the old golden bytes decode as a one-type view. [Task 10]
14. **No Werewolves, no Tensura, no bosses.** A coven-and-clan group forms, judges joins, releases and elects without Werewolves (setup 2); the NPC rule is only present with Tensura (setup 6 and 14); nothing here imports `bosses`. [Tasks 6, 7, 9, 13]
