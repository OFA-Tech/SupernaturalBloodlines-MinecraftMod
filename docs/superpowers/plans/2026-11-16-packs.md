# Plan 14: packs (mixed packs, Alpha Packs, allies, party link)

Branch `feature/packs`. Source of the rules: the owner's decisions of 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16 (see the memory notes on plans 14/15). Existing rules that stay: R90 to R94 of `SPECIES-RULES.md`, the True Alpha keeps the spark (R91), a stolen spark, `PackIndex` loading old data leniently.

## Model

- A **pack** is a leader plus members, kept in `PackIndex` (`member -> leader`). Nothing about size is limited.
- A member is a **Beta** (a werewolf, as before), a **Packmate** (anyone who is not a werewolf: any species, kept in the index only, no `WolfState` change) or a **co-Alpha** (a second Alpha; their `WolfState.packAlpha` stays empty, the index says whose pack they are in).
- The **leader** is the strongest Alpha of the pack: True Alpha before Alpha, then the highest Werewolves level, then the sitting leader (so a tie never flips it). The index remembers each member's last seen strength, so an offline Alpha still counts.
- Recalculated on join, leave, kick, level change and login. When the leader loses the spark, dies by a stolen spark or leaves the werewolves, the strongest co-Alpha takes over before any Beta is made Alpha.
- Only the leader invites and kicks. Co-Alphas are admins (OPAC rank ADMIN).
- **Allies** are two packs, by their leaders: no damage between them, an Alpha Roar does not force-shift their members, no shared perks. Symmetric; either leader ends it.

## Tasks

1. **Pure rules** (`core/bloodline`): `PackLeadership.pick` (strength order, tie keeps the sitting leader), a `Strength` record with a packed key. JUnit.
2. **Index**: `leaderOf`, `pack`, `strength` map, `kick`, `rekey`, `merge` (an Alpha with a pack joining another brings their members), `allies`. NBT with every new field optional (old files load). `follow` and `login` leave Alphas and Packmates alone. JUnit on the plain parts, GameTest on the world data.
3. **Mixed packs and `/pack kick`**: `invite` accepts any player who is not in a pack (also Alphas), `accept` joins as Beta, Packmate or co-Alpha, `leave` works for them, `kick` by name (online or offline, `GameProfileArgument`), leader only. `PackBonus`, `AlphaRoar`, `Emissary`, `DefenderKills`, the hub read the whole pack through the index, so a Packmate has every perk.
4. **Alpha Packs**: leader recalculation, the death or spark-loss handover to the strongest co-Alpha, killing an Alpha of your own pack still steals the spark, `AlphaTrial.win` (the stub for plan 15: a Beta who wins becomes a second Alpha in the same pack).
5. **Allies**: `/pack ally invite|accept|remove`, no damage between allied packs, the roar skips their forced shift.
6. **Party link** (`parts/species/pack/party`): a `PartyBackend` interface, the OPAC implementation by reflection (the jar is not a build dependency, so nothing changes without the mod and the same code serves Team Claims, which has the same mod id and API), a no-op backend when the mod is absent. The pack mirrors to a party: owner = the founding leader, other Alphas = ADMIN, everyone else MEMBER. A member who left the party leaves the pack; a party that was disbanded is not recreated until the roster changes. Tested with a fake backend; the reflection checked against the jar with javap and, if cheap, a real run.
7. **Hub Pack page** in all three themes: leader crown, Alphas, Packmates, allies, kick and ally actions with confirmations, built from the kit.
8. **Docs, wiki, changelog, memory notes**; matrix; devshots of the Pack page in the three themes.
