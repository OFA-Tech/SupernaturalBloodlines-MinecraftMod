# Tensura addons in the OFA "Holy Packs" pack

Audit of the 33 addons the owner named, against `origin/main` (5a33b7ad, after PR #67) and the PR #68 branch (`feature/plan-18d`, 0ba3e77b: Rip Heart, Neck Snap). It follows PR #63 (the first Holy Packs audit) and only repeats what main changed since: Augustines, the turning and the wake, the HUD, mastery, the Enchanted rites, the Klaus sire, the Tensura races layer, naming and subordinates.

Pack: NeoForge 21.1.267, ManasCore 4.0.0.2, Tensura 2.0.1.5. The pack has **neither Vampirism nor Werewolves**: our mod brings them, so the boots below add Vampirism 1.21-1.10.13 and Werewolves 1.21-2.0.3.4.

**Tensura Slots (`tensuraslots-1.0.0`) is removed from the pack on release (owner decision); our HUD replaces it.** It is not audited. PR #63's support for running both stays as it is.

## How it was checked

- **javap and mixin scan** of every jar (`mixscan.py`: parses the `@Mixin` targets and every injector of every mixin config; `evscan.py`: every NeoForge/Architectury event listener). Our targets are in `docs/mixins.md`.
- **Data scan** of every `data/` tree: ids in our namespaces, `replace: true` tags, duplicate paths between addons.
- **Real boots**, headless dedicated server, no client, logs only in `%TEMP%\opencode\compat-tensura`:
  - (b) the pack as it is (Slots in, no Vampirism, no our jar): the baseline. Done in 26 s; 845 WARN and 329 ERROR, all of them the pack's own (missing refmaps and optional mixin targets, Railways loot tables for absent wood mods, Sable `copycats`, TagLoader for Supplementaries, and an `OutOfMemoryError` in `infinity:vormir` under `-Xmx6G`).
  - (a) the pack without Slots + Vampirism + Werewolves + **our main jar**: Done in 30 s. All five of our mixin plugins applied and every hook reported in place (`RiteCostMixin`, `RiteCompleteMixin`, `VampireRaceFeedMixin`, `AbilityStorageNextPresetMixin`, `RaceInstanceMixin`); all 11 parts enabled; `bloodline` commands answer. **Nothing at WARN or ERROR is ours.** What (a) adds over (b) is Vampirism and Werewolves (their own `getHiddenEffect` mixin overwrite conflict, mob categories in biomes).
  - (c) the same with the **PR #68 jar**: identical to (a) (the branch adds no mixin and no registry entry).
- **GameTests with the real addons** (`-PaddonJars=<folder>`, a dev aid added to `build.gradle`): 39 pack jars on the run classpath (the addons, what they need, and the Rebalanced modules unpacked from `tensura_rebalanced_combined`). Not loadable in a GameTest server, so covered by boot and javap only: `infinity-stones-tensura` (needs NeoForge 21.1.266 or newer, the dev environment is .265), `tensura_opac` (OPAC cannot start without a profile cache), `tensuraslots`.

## Fixed (ours, with tests)

1. **A chosen death (Give up, or a Rip Heart: the owner rule is that a heart-rip is final and overrides other mods' revives too, like the Totem, the Elixir and the Ring) must happen whatever saves from death** (`core/death/FinalDeath`). `FinalDeath.is` is asked by our own savers (the Elixir, the Resurrection Ring), but not by addon skills: Re:Zero's Return by Death, Infinity Stones' Immortality, Return Pact, Horizons' Phoenix, Ascension's talents cancel `LivingDeathEvent` and heal. A player who gave up in the dying state (the turning, an Original) could be brought back by one. A listener at `LOW` (registered in the mod constructor, so before the parts' own LOW listeners: marks, sparks, genes, the transition must see a final death) and another at `LOWEST`, both receiving cancelled events, now un-cancel a death that happens inside `FinalDeath.run` and set the health to 0. What the overridden save already did stays (a charge paid, a teleport, a cooldown). A vampire whose death Vampirism caught at HIGHEST died with its dying state still set: `RipHeartAction` now resets it (`OriginalRules.clearDyingState`, only `giveUpDBNO`, no second `die`, so no double loot). **Vampirism 1.10.13 does not keep the dying state through a respawn** (javap: `VampirePlayer.serializeNBT` writes only `wasDBNO`; the `dbno` timer is not serialized and a new body starts at -1), so a stale state was never a trap for the next life, only for a corpse's listeners. Registered once in the mod constructor, no mixin. Tests: `FinalDeathSaverGameTests` (a fake saver at NORMAL saves an ordinary death, and does not save a chosen one), `TargetedSkillGameTests.ripHeartDeathReachesTheLowListenersWhateverSavedItAtNormal` (a LOW listener still sees the death) and `ripHeartFinishesAVampireWhoseDeathVampirismWouldCatch` (asserts `!isDBNO()`).
2. **Grants work with Mysticism** (`SkillOwners.grant`): sets Mysticism's one-shot `NoSoulEnergyCost` next to `NoMagiculeCost` and removes it again from the held skill. Without Mysticism the tag is never read and never stays. Test: `RankSkillsGameTests.aGrantLeavesNoSoulEnergySwitchBehind`; with the addons on the classpath `truealphagetsthecommanderskill` and `dormanttruealphaskillsreturnonrejoin` pass again.
3. **Horizons' rung** is part of the saint tier (`ApexLines.HUMAN_LIKE`; `HumanLikeLine.SAINT_OR_HIGHER` is now derived from it, one list). Test: `HumanLikeLineTest`.
3b. **Rip Heart and Neck Snap leave other addons' bosses alone** (`snap_immune.json`: `#c:bosses`, the vanilla dragon and wither, Milim, Leon, Halo Vessel; PR #68 was merged while this audit ran).
4. **Key tests** pin more of the pack's live binds (`HudKeysDefaultsTest`).
5. Dev aid: `-PaddonJars=<folder>` in `build.gradle`.

## Subordinates and naming

- Nothing in the 33 changes `SubordinateHelper.isSubordinate`/`getSubordinateOwnerUUID`, `RequestNamingMenuPacket.name` or the `playerNaming` rule. `SirelineTensuraGameTests` (sire naming, subordinates, severance) pass with all addons on the classpath.
- **Better Subordinates** keeps its own storage for every entity that has a Tensura subordinate owner and ticks it. It grants a subordinate extra skills by kills, damage taken and the owner's EP. For a **player** (our sired) the skill menu and the manipulation of skills are off by default (`subordinatePlayerSkillMenu = false`, `subordinatePlayerSkillManipulation = false` in its config). Recommendation: keep both false; with them on a sired player would be handled like a pet with a skill menu, and its tallies could grant them Tensura skills outside our layer.
- Tensura OPAC's `isAlly` (party members are allies) is the only addon touch on `SubordinateHelper`; it only adds `true`.

## Their side, and owner decisions

1. **Bosses of other addons are not "bosses" for Rip Heart and Neck Snap (PR #68).** `TargetChecks.isBoss` is `#supernaturalbloodlines:snap_immune` or `AlphaTrialBoss`: only our bosses. `c:bosses` (already read by `LinkingRiteFactory`) holds Tensura's, Re:Zero's, NEB's and Diablo, but **Milim (`foxboss:milim`), Leon (`foxboss_leon_cromwell:leon_cromwell`) and Ascension's `ascension:halo_vessel` are in no tag**. **Fixed here after #68 merged:** `snap_immune.json` now also lists `#c:bosses`, `minecraft:ender_dragon`, `minecraft:wither`, `foxboss:milim`, `foxboss_leon_cromwell:leon_cromwell` and `ascension:halo_vessel`, all `required: false` (the tag test skips entries of other mods).
2. **Tensura Rebalanced core and lethal environmental damage** (see the table). Pack decision: if the owner wants lava and fire to stay lethal for the Downed/turning rules, look for the environmental-damage switch in its `tensura_rebalanced/environment.toml` (found by javap in `EnvironmentalDamage.register`, the keys were not read). Our tests that use a lava or fire killing blow cannot run with it on.
3. **Rebalanced modules in GameTests**: the `Connection.channel()` NullPointerException in five of our disconnect/rejoin tests with the Rebalanced core present comes from its own login listener meeting a test player without a network channel; a real player always has one. Not a problem in the game; it is why those five tests are not run against the Rebalanced jars.
4. **Great Sage**: its default `assess` key is V (our slot 3) and its recolour of text may tint our HUD. Needs a look in a client by the owner, with the pack's keymap. Keep the pack's keymap in the release notes.
5. **Horizons' `HorizonHudMixin` and Milim/Leon's own boss-bar overlays** are client drawing; our `BloodInFoodSpot` reads Tensura's `OverlayHandler` positions and `BossBars` reads vanilla's boss-bar event. If the owner sets a custom HUD anchor in Horizons, or fights Milim or Leon (their own overlay), the blood pill may overlap. Not checked without a client.
6. **Pre-existing in the pack**: `tensura_ascension_rebalanced` targets a class Ascension 3.1.0 no longer has; `infinity:vormir` runs out of heap with `-Xmx6G` on a dedicated server (seen in the baseline too); `copycats:copycat_catwalk` unknown to Sable.
7. **Infinity Stones needs NeoForge 21.1.266 or newer**; the pack has .267; our build still compiles against .265 (fine for us).
