# Part: bosses

Requires: **vampirism and enchanted** (the species part's lines, which the NPC Originals root) and **GeckoLib** (Enchanted already requires it, so it costs nobody anything). Needs the `species` part. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-bosses.md`. This is phase **17c**: the NPC Original base, **Mikael** (17b) and **Klaus** (17c). Elijah, Kol, Rebekah, Finn and the werewolf bosses come in 17d to 17h; their switches (`<boss>_enabled`) already exist and do nothing yet.

**Mixin budget: 0 of 3** (see `docs/mixins.md`).

## What it adds

- The first **entity types** of the mod: `supernaturalbloodlines:mikael`. A plain `PathfinderMob` (not a Vampirism entity: no sun, no bite, no conversion, no Vampirism AI), never despawns, has a boss bar for players within 64 blocks. Server and client load the same classes; nothing under `parts/bosses` except `client/` names a client class (`BossServerSafetyTest` reads every import).
- **`BossIndex`** (SavedData `supernaturalbloodlines_bosses`, in the overworld's data storage): for each boss its state (`UNSEEN`, `ACTIVE`, `OPEN`, `DEAD`, `LOST`), the entity's uuid, its last dimension and place, the day it returns, whether its reward was paid, how many times it came, the day its blood may drop again, the last night rolled, and who it hunts. A bad entry is skipped, not the file.
- **`BossDirector`**: one `LevelTickEvent.Post` hook on the overworld, every `boss_check_seconds`. It marks a boss **lost** when its entity is gone from a loaded chunk for two checks in a row (an unloaded chunk says nothing), and rolls for Mikael. No vanilla spawn rules, no biome modifier. Every refusal is one table, `SpawnRules`, in this order: `disabled`, `needs_mod`, `dead`, `present`, `cooling`.
- **The NPC Original base** (`NpcOriginalBoss`): carries `ORIGINAL_ROOT` (so the line rules, `LineDeath`, `LineWarning`, the Imprisoned Original rite and Revelation treat it like a player Original) and `NPC_OPEN` (the Imprisoned Original rite's "brought down"). What a blow does is `DefeatRules` (R151), played by `NpcOriginalEvents`.
- **Mikael**: black clothes, his white oak stake in his hand. Once a night, with the chance `mikael_night_chance`, he comes for one vampire player (never an Original or an Original hybrid, R158) of at least `mikael_min_rung`, on the ground, in the overworld, outside a mountain ash ring, 24 to 40 blocks away (6 to 24 when there is no ground, or no loaded chunk, that far). The target is told ("A hunter in black is watching you", with his name only when they have discovered him, R123). He feeds on the vampires he hits (a quarter of the damage, R156). Peaceful: he never comes. If his prey is gone for a minute he withdraws and can come again.
- **Mikael's Stake** (`mikaels_stake`): his reward and weapon. In `#supernaturalbloodlines:white_oak`, never breaks.
- **Klaus** (`supernaturalbloodlines:klaus`, the first GeckoLib form): one entity with a synced form, `HUMAN`, `SHIFTING`, `BEAST`, so his UUID, health, boss bar and index entry never change when he shifts. The man is the player model (`textures/entity/boss/klaus.png`); the wolf is GeckoLib (`geo/entity/klaus_hybrid.geo.json`, six animations, the glow mask on the golden irises). `ShiftRenderer` (client only) picks the renderer by the form every frame: the man, then from half way through the shift the wolf. The hitbox follows the form (0.6 x 1.95, then 0.9 x 1.5). He shifts once, at half health or against a werewolf, and stays a wolf: +50% damage, +25% speed. **Needs Werewolves** (`modsPresent`): without it the director never sends him, and the command refuses with "a mod it needs is missing". His entity type still registers, so a world that adds Werewolves later works.
- **The hunt**: on a full moon night (`MoonRule`), with the chance `klaus_chance_per_full_moon` (0.35), once a night, for a doppelgänger: a villager that is one and has a player within 64 blocks, or (with `klaus_hunts_players`, on) a player who carries the gene. A player is warned `klaus_warning_seconds` (90) before he arrives, on the action bar and in chat, with a howl and "Mountain ash will hold him."; a villager is never warned and he comes at once. He arrives 24 to 40 blocks away (6 to 24 when there is no ground that far). If his prey is gone and he fights nobody for a minute he withdraws and may come again. The warning is kept in the index (`arriveAt`), so a restart does not lose it.
- **Klaus's Blood** (`klaus_blood`): his reward. Counts as the blood of an Original hybrid under either `hybrid_transition_blood`, so a werewolf who drinks it and dies starts the transition in Klaus's line, and the transition death still applies. It also cures werewolf venom, like Hybrid's Blood. It is an item, not a Sire's Blood bottle.

## The rules

| Rule | What | Test |
|---|---|---|
| R150 | The states of a boss. `DEAD` is final; only `FORGET` leaves it. A lost boss may be brought back. | `BossRulesTest` |
| R151 | White oak kills an NPC Original only while they are open (`npc_original_stake_needs_open`, on by default); on a standing one it hurts but cannot take the last health. Anything else lethal, and the Ash Dagger, desiccates a standing one (health 1, no AI, open, the line warned, their blood dropped). An open one cannot be hurt below 1 by anything but white oak. `/kill` and the void end them. | `DefeatRulesTest`, `NpcOriginalGameTests` |
| R152 | A desiccated Original gets up after `original_return_days` days (at least 1), with full health; the line is told "...has risen. Your line is safe again." (only if it had been warned) and is warned again the next time. **The days are days of game time** (`getGameTime() / 24000`, `BossClock`), not of the time of day: sleeping through the nights or `/time set` does not shorten them. The check is the entity's own, so one that was in an unloaded chunk gets up when it loads; one that is loaded desiccated after a restart warns the line again. | `DesiccationRulesTest`, `NpcOriginalGameTests`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R153 | A boss pays its reward once (`boss_reward_on`: `first_defeat` = on desiccation or death, `death` = only on death). A return does not pay again; a lost boss that comes back does not either. | `RewardRulesTest`, `NpcOriginalGameTests` |
| R154 | The refusals of the director, in order. | `SpawnRulesTest` |
| R155 | Who Mikael hunts: a vampire of at least the rung, at night (ticks 13000 to 22999), in the overworld, on the ground, outside an ash ring; one roll a night, below the chance. | `MikaelRulesTest`, `MikaelGameTests` |
| R156 | He heals a quarter of the damage he deals to a vampire. | `MikaelRulesTest` |
| R157 | A white oak death is for good: the index says `DEAD`, the line dies with them (`LineDeath`, if `original_line_death` is on, and only when the Original was desiccated or `npc_original_stake_needs_open` is off), they never come back until an operator resets them. A lost boss that dies is dead too. | `NpcOriginalGameTests`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R158 | **A mob's white oak hit never counts as a white oak kill** (`OriginalRules.isWhiteOak` needs a player, or a mob that `OriginalRules.mobWhiteOakCounts` allows). So Mikael cannot kill a player Original outright, cannot finish one who is dying, and cannot start line death. He does not hunt Originals or Original hybrids (`MikaelRules.eligible`, `NightHuntGoal`). `mikael_stakes_originals` (off) brings it back for him alone: he hunts them, his stake kills them, the line dies, and the hunted player is told "His stake is white oak. If he kills you, your whole line dies with you." | `MikaelReviewRulesTest`, `BossReviewGameTests`, `OriginalGameTests` |
| R159 | Feedback: white oak on a standing Original says "White oak only works on an Original who is down." on the action bar, at most once every 60 ticks for each player (`NoticeRules`); the boss bar of a fallen Original is yellow and reads "Mikael (desiccated)"; the hunt warning is on the action bar and in chat, with a sound and "Mountain ash will hold him." | `NoticeRulesTest`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R160 | A lost boss that shows itself again is found again, standing (`ACTIVE`) or lying (`OPEN`); the old one that comes back after a new one was made is discarded (`BossIndex.seen`). Mikael withdraws (discarded, the index says lost, so the director may send him again) when his prey has been gone, or it has been day with nobody to fight, for 1200 ticks; a fallen Original never withdraws. The Ash ring is searched only for a player who passes everything else. The `MISSES` count is forgotten when the server stops. A paid reward is written to disk at once. | `MikaelReviewRulesTest`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R161 | **The shift** (`ShiftRules`): only the human form shifts, once, at half health or less (alive) or when the one he fights is a werewolf; it takes 40 ticks (the man shows for the first half, the wolf from then on), he stands still meanwhile, and a howl plays for 64 ticks. The hitbox follows the body. The form, the ticks and the beast's bonuses survive a save. | `ShiftRulesTest`, `KlausGameTests` |
| R162 | **Klaus's hunt** (`KlausRules`): who may be hunted (a doppelgänger, in the overworld, not in spectator; a villager only near a player; a player only with `klaus_hunts_players`), the roll (a roll of exactly the chance does not pass), the warning (a player only), the arrival that was put off, the withdrawal. One roll a night, spent only when somebody could be hunted. | `KlausRulesTest`, `KlausGameTests` |
| R163 | **The bite**: venom (the `hybrid_venom` effect, not renewed on a victim who has it) only in the beast form and only on a vampire. | `KlausRulesTest`, `KlausGameTests` |
| R164 | **Klaus's Blood** is judged as an Original hybrid's (`KlausRules.BLOOD_SPECIES`), so it starts the hybrid transition under either setting; it records Klaus as the source; it cures venom. | `TransitionBloodTest`, `KlausGameTests` |

White oak working only while the Original is open is the rule the plan named; the stake and the Ash Dagger are the two tools of the story: the dagger opens them, the stake ends them.

## Where it is in the hub

- **Admin page, section Bosses** (operators only): a boss picker and the five commands as buttons: Spawn here (X1), State (X2), Desiccate (X3), Return now (X4), Forget (X5, asks first). The settings (`boss_check_seconds`, `boss_bars_enabled`, `original_return_days`, `npc_original_stake_needs_open`, `boss_reward_on`, `mikael_enabled`, and the other eight `<boss>_enabled`, `boss_health_factor`, `boss_damage_factor`, `mikael_min_rung`, `mikael_night_chance`, `mikael_stakes_originals`, `klaus_chance_per_full_moon`, `klaus_hunts_players`, `klaus_warning_seconds`) are on the same page with the other settings (`AdminEntries`; `AdminCoverageTest` fails when a key has no row).
- **Guide**: a chapter "The bosses", last, shown when the part is on, in short paragraphs. It says plainly that if your Original dies to white oak you die too (and so does everyone in their line), gives the rung (3), and ends with a button to the Bloodline page.
- **Home, "The world now"**: a crimson diamond line when a boss is out there within 128 blocks, a steel-blue one ("An Original lies fallen nearby.") when an Original lies fallen; named only to a player who has discovered them (`WorldNowHook.BossNear`, the snapshot's `bossNear` and `bossName`; the hub protocol is now 7).
- Admin labels: "Boss: Mikael" (and the other eight), "NPC Original stake needs a desiccated target", `mikael_stakes_originals`.

## Commands

`/supernaturalbloodlines boss <boss> spawn|state|desiccate|return|reset` (permission 2). `<boss>` suggests only the bosses that have landed (Mikael and Klaus). `spawn` refuses with the reason from `SpawnRules` (a boss that is already out there is not made twice, and Klaus is not made without Werewolves).

## Layout

- `core/boss/` (pure): `BossId`, `BossState`, `BossRules`, `DefeatRules`, `DesiccationRules`, `RewardRules`, `BossFacts`, `SpawnRules`, `BossWeight`, `MikaelRules`, `BossForm`, `ShiftRules`, `KlausRules`.
- `parts/bosses/`: `BossesPart`, `BossesConfig`, `BossRegistries` (entity types, attributes), `BossItems`, `item/KlausBloodItem`.
- `parts/bosses/director/`: `BossIndex`, `BossDirector`, `BossBars`. `entity/`: `BossNpc`, `NpcOriginalBoss`, `MikaelEntity`, `KlausEntity` (a `GeoEntity`: only GeckoLib's common animation types), `NpcOriginalEvents`, `ai/NightHuntGoal`, `ai/PreyGoal`. `command/BossCommand`. `client/`: `BossRenderers`, `HumanoidBossRenderer` (player model with `textures/entity/boss/<id>.png`; a desiccated one lies flat), `ShiftRenderer` (the man or the wolf by form), `BossGeoModel`, `BossShots`.
- Hooks elsewhere: `SpeciesRegistries.NPC_OPEN` and `Imprisonment.isDown`, `WorldNowHook.setBossNear`, `GuideChapters`, `AdminPage`/`AdminEntries`/`AdminCommands`.

## Tests

- Pure: `BossIdTest`, `BossRulesTest`, `DefeatRulesTest`, `DesiccationRulesTest`, `RewardRulesTest`, `SpawnRulesTest`, `MikaelRulesTest`, `BossServerSafetyTest`, and the guide, admin and wire tests that changed with the part. Each was shown to fail with its rule reverted (a script of mutants, listed in the PR).
- GameTests: `NpcOriginalGameTests`, `MikaelGameTests`, `BossReviewGameTests`, `KlausGameTests` (the director, the warning, the shift and the hitbox, the bite, the blood, line death, the withdrawal, the command), `BossServerGameTests` (4), `BossesPartOffGameTests` (1: off leaves no entity, item or command).
- **Dedicated server (U6)**: the GameTest run is a dedicated server (`FMLEnvironment.dist == DEDICATED_SERVER`, no client classes), and `BossServerGameTests` spawns Mikael and Klaus, ticks them, shifts Klaus, and saves and loads them there. **The GeckoLib renderer loads only on the client**: the same GameTest tries to load `net.minecraft.client.Minecraft`, GeckoLib's `GeoEntityRenderer` and our `ShiftRenderer` on the server and each is refused (the dist cleaner), while `KlausEntity` and GeckoLib's `GeoEntity` load; `BossServerSafetyTest` adds that only `client/` names GeckoLib's renderer or model, that nothing else names `parts.bosses.client` but `BossesPart`, and that `BossesPart` does it only inside the `Dist.CLIENT` guard.
- Hidden screenshots: `-Dsupernaturalbloodlines.devshots.bosses=mikael` or `=klaus` (`BossShots`), with `runSafeShots`: Mikael from the front, the side, behind and desiccated; Klaus as a man from the front and behind, half way through the shift, and as a wolf from the front and the side. See `docs/safe-screenshots.md`.
