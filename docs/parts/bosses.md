# Part: bosses

Requires: **vampirism and enchanted** (the species part's lines, which the NPC Originals root) and **GeckoLib** (Enchanted already requires it, so it costs nobody anything). Needs the `species` part. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-bosses.md`. This is phase **17d**: the NPC Original base, **Mikael** (17b), **Klaus** (17c), **Elijah and Rebekah** (17d). Elijah, Kol, Rebekah, Finn and the werewolf bosses come in 17d to 17h; their switches (`<boss>_enabled`) already exist and do nothing yet.

**Mixin budget: 0 of 3** (see `docs/mixins.md`).

## What it adds

- The first **entity types** of the mod: `supernaturalbloodlines:mikael`. A plain `PathfinderMob` (not a Vampirism entity: no sun, no bite, no conversion, no Vampirism AI), never despawns, has a boss bar for players within 64 blocks. Server and client load the same classes; nothing under `parts/bosses` except `client/` names a client class (`BossServerSafetyTest` reads every import).
- **`BossIndex`** (SavedData `supernaturalbloodlines_bosses`, in the overworld's data storage): for each boss its state (`UNSEEN`, `ACTIVE`, `OPEN`, `DEAD`, `LOST`), the entity's uuid, its last dimension and place, the day it returns, whether its reward was paid, how many times it came, the day its blood may drop again, the last night rolled, and who it hunts. A bad entry is skipped, not the file.
- **`BossDirector`**: one `LevelTickEvent.Post` hook on the overworld, every `boss_check_seconds`. It marks a boss **lost** when its entity is gone from a loaded chunk for two checks in a row (an unloaded chunk says nothing), and rolls for Mikael. No vanilla spawn rules, no biome modifier. Every refusal is one table, `SpawnRules`, in this order: `disabled`, `needs_mod`, `dead`, `present`, `cooling`.
- **The NPC Original base** (`NpcOriginalBoss`): carries `ORIGINAL_ROOT` (so the line rules, `LineDeath`, `LineWarning`, the Imprisoned Original rite and Revelation treat it like a player Original) and `NPC_OPEN` (the Imprisoned Original rite's "brought down"). What a blow does is `DefeatRules` (R151), played by `NpcOriginalEvents`.
- **Mikael**: black clothes, his white oak stake in his hand. Once a night, with the chance `mikael_night_chance`, he comes for one vampire player (never an Original or an Original hybrid, R158) of at least `mikael_min_rung`, on the ground, in the overworld, outside a mountain ash ring, 24 to 40 blocks away (6 to 24 when there is no ground, or no loaded chunk, that far). The target is told ("A hunter in black is watching you", with his name only when they have discovered him, R123). He feeds on the vampires he hits (a quarter of the damage, R156). Peaceful: he never comes. If his prey is gone for a minute he withdraws and can come again. **A plain mountain ash line does not hold him**: `MountainAshLineBlock.blocks` lets a line stop the mobs of `#supernaturalbloodlines:supernatural_mobs` only when a pack Emissary laid it (R166, a GameTest shows both), so every text says "a mountain ash line laid by a pack Emissary".
- **Mikael's Stake** (`mikaels_stake`): his reward and weapon. In `#supernaturalbloodlines:white_oak`, never breaks.
- **Klaus** (`supernaturalbloodlines:klaus`, the first GeckoLib form): one entity with a synced form, `HUMAN`, `SHIFTING`, `BEAST`, so his UUID, health, boss bar and index entry never change when he shifts. The man is the player model (`textures/entity/boss/klaus.png`); the wolf is GeckoLib (`geo/entity/klaus_hybrid.geo.json`, six animations, the glow mask on the golden irises). `ShiftRenderer` (client only) picks the renderer by the form every frame: the man, then from half way through the shift the wolf. The hitbox follows the body that is drawn (0.6 x 1.95, then 0.9 x 1.5, from the swap tick). He shifts once, at half health or against a werewolf, **never while he lies desiccated**, and stays a wolf: +50% damage, +25% speed. A desiccated wolf plays and holds the death animation. **Needs Werewolves** (`modsPresent`): without it the director never sends him, and the command refuses with "a mod it needs is missing". His entity type still registers, so a world that adds Werewolves later works.
- **The hunt**: on a full moon night (`MoonRule`), with the chance `klaus_chance_per_full_moon` (0.35), once a night, for a doppelgänger: a villager that is one and has a player within 64 blocks, or (with `klaus_hunts_players`, on) a player who carries the gene. A player is warned `klaus_warning_seconds` (90) before he arrives, on the action bar and in chat, with a howl that **only that player hears** (`playNotifySound`) and a hint that names the threat (a hybrid wolf, venom bite); the action bar repeats a short line at 30 and 10 seconds. **Being hunted tells the player they carry the doppelgänger gene** (R165). A villager is never warned and he comes at once. He arrives 24 to 40 blocks away (6 to 24 when there is no ground that far). The warning is kept in the index (`arriveAt`, `reminded`), so a restart does not lose it, and it is asked again at every look: if the player dies, leaves, changes dimension, goes to spectator, the player hunt is switched off or the world is made peaceful, the arrival is dropped at once and a player who is still here is told "The wolf lost your scent."
- **Klaus's Blood** (`klaus_blood`): his reward. Counts as the blood of an Original hybrid under either `hybrid_transition_blood`, so a werewolf who drinks it and dies starts the transition in Klaus's line, and the transition death still applies. It also cures werewolf venom, like Hybrid's Blood, and says so in its tooltip. If Klaus's line is dead (`LineIndex.isDead`) it still cures but marks nobody, and says so. It is an item, not a Sire's Blood bottle.
- **Elijah** (`supernaturalbloodlines:elijah`, calm): once a day, with the chance `elijah_chance_per_day` (0.05), by day or by night (and in a peaceful world), he comes 8 to 20 blocks from a player who has discovered an Original or carries the blood of an NPC Original (R170). The visit names him only to someone who has discovered him. He fights only the one who hit him, until an oath is broken: then he is hostile for good. **Right-click opens the deal** (R167): a choice dialog in the hub's own look (`ElijahDealScreen`: the player's hub theme, the kit's `ChoiceLayout` and `ChoiceModel`, not a hub page). It says what you pick, the oath, for how many in-game days, and what breaking it costs; its buttons scroll when they do not fit (GUI scale 4 at 720p) and it is read out by the narrator. It lists every NPC Original but Elijah, the same for everybody: leaving one out would tell a player which line they are in. Picking the Original whose line you are in is allowed (the vial has a name on it and nothing more). The answer is that Original's id; the server checks it against the list it would offer and says why on the action bar when it refuses. He stands still, facing the player, while an offer is open (60 seconds). The vial is a Sire's Blood bottle of that Original, and the player swears an oath for `elijah_oath_days` (7) in-game days: not to harm or desiccate any NPC Original but Mikael, and not to strike Elijah. A player is offered one deal, ever; declining leaves no record. A blow to Elijah, Klaus or Rebekah while the oath binds breaks it, writes a broken record, makes Elijah hostile for good (`BossIndex.Entry.hostile`) and removes the deal for everybody. A tamed animal or a Vampirism minion strikes for its owner (the oath is about what you set on them too), unless the Original was already after that owner or had been hurt by them: then it is defending them and nothing counts; any other mob's blow never breaks anything.
- **The Elixir** (`the_elixir`): Elijah's reward. Carried in the inventory, it brings its holder back from the next death that is not a line death, not `/kill`, not the white oak end of an Original, not a death that is part of becoming something (vampire blood in the veins, an unstable hybrid transition, Original blood) and not the void (the tooltip and the Guide say so), once, at `elixir_heal_fraction` (0.5) of their health (R168). A death the Resurrection Ring would save is left to the ring (`RingProbe`).
- **Rebekah** (`supernaturalbloodlines:rebekah`, slim skin): the Rite of the Cure fires `CureMadeEvent` (U2) when it makes a Cure; `rebekah_delay_seconds` (60) later she arrives 12 to 48 blocks from the circle and hunts whoever cast it (R169). She does not answer a Cure made while she lies desiccated, is dead, is out there already or is switched off, a `/give` or a dropped Cure is no event, and she never comes into a peaceful world. With nobody left to hunt for a minute she withdraws and may come again. The one who made the Cure is told at once "The rite is done. Something old has noticed."; the one she hunts is told "Rebekah is here" only if they have discovered her, else "Someone is here". A mountain ash line laid by a pack Emissary holds her and Elijah, a plain one does not (GameTest, like Klaus and Mikael: R166).
- **The True Cure** (`the_true_cure`): Rebekah's reward. One drink clears every layer (`DRANK_THE_TRUE_CURE`, the same change as The Cure, R171); an Original drinks it willingly with no rite and no desiccation, and the ones they sired keep their line (a cured Original is not a line death).

## The rules

| Rule | What | Test |
|---|---|---|
| R150 | The states of a boss. `DEAD` is final; only `FORGET` leaves it. A lost boss may be brought back. | `BossRulesTest` |
| R151 | White oak kills an NPC Original only while they are open (`npc_original_stake_needs_open`, on by default); on a standing one it hurts but cannot take the last health. Anything else lethal, and the Ash Dagger, desiccates a standing one (health 1, no AI, open, the line warned, their blood dropped). An open one cannot be hurt below 1 by anything but white oak. `/kill` and the void end them. | `DefeatRulesTest`, `NpcOriginalGameTests` |
| R152 | A desiccated Original gets up after `original_return_days` days (at least 1), with full health; the line is told "...has risen. Your line is safe again." (only if it had been warned) and is warned again the next time. **The days are days of game time** (`getGameTime() / 24000`, `BossClock`), not of the time of day: sleeping through the nights or `/time set` does not shorten them. The check is the entity's own, so one that was in an unloaded chunk gets up when it loads; one that is loaded desiccated after a restart warns the line again. | `DesiccationRulesTest`, `NpcOriginalGameTests`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R153 | A boss pays its reward once (`boss_reward_on`: `first_defeat` = on desiccation or death, `death` = only on death). A return does not pay again; a lost boss that comes back does not either. | `RewardRulesTest`, `NpcOriginalGameTests` |
| R154 | The refusals of the director, in order. | `SpawnRulesTest` |
| R155 | Who Mikael hunts: a vampire of at least the rung, at night (ticks 13000 to 22999), in the overworld, on the ground, outside an ash ring; one roll a night, below the chance. | `MikaelRulesTest`, `MikaelGameTests` |
| R156 | He heals a quarter of the damage he deals to a vampire. | `MikaelRulesTest` |
| R157 | A white oak death is for good (the Elixir does not keep an Original from it: R168): the index says `DEAD`, the line dies with them (`LineDeath`, if `original_line_death` is on, and only when the Original was desiccated or `npc_original_stake_needs_open` is off), they never come back until an operator resets them. A lost boss that dies is dead too. | `NpcOriginalGameTests`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R158 | **A mob's white oak hit never counts as a white oak kill** (`OriginalRules.isWhiteOak` needs a player, or a mob that `OriginalRules.mobWhiteOakCounts` allows). So Mikael cannot kill a player Original outright, cannot finish one who is dying, and cannot start line death. He does not hunt Originals or Original hybrids (`MikaelRules.eligible`, `NightHuntGoal`). `mikael_stakes_originals` (off) brings it back for him alone: he hunts them, his stake kills them, the line dies, and the hunted player is told "His stake is white oak. If he kills you, your whole line dies with you." | `MikaelReviewRulesTest`, `BossReviewGameTests`, `OriginalGameTests` |
| R159 | Feedback: white oak on a standing Original says "White oak only works on an Original who is down." on the action bar, at most once every 60 ticks for each player (`NoticeRules`); the boss bar of a fallen Original is yellow and reads "Mikael (desiccated)"; the hunt warning is on the action bar and in chat, with a sound and "A mountain ash line laid by a pack Emissary will hold him." (R166 made the text true) | `NoticeRulesTest`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R160 | A lost boss that shows itself again is found again, standing (`ACTIVE`) or lying (`OPEN`); the old one that comes back after a new one was made is discarded (`BossIndex.seen`). Mikael withdraws (discarded, the index says lost, so the director may send him again) when his prey has been gone, or it has been day with nobody to fight, for 1200 ticks; a fallen Original never withdraws. The Ash ring is searched only for a player who passes everything else. The `MISSES` count is forgotten when the server stops. A paid reward is written to disk at once. | `MikaelReviewRulesTest`, `BossReviewGameTests` |
| R161 | **The shift** (`ShiftRules`): only the human form shifts, once, at half health or less (alive) or when the one he fights is a werewolf, and never while he is desiccated; it takes 40 ticks and he stands still meanwhile. **On tick 20 (`SWAP_TICK`) the body is swapped**: the wolf model and hitbox from that tick, a burst of smoke, a howl (the howl animation starts then and runs 64 ticks) and a short shake. The form, the ticks and the beast's bonuses survive a save. | `ShiftRulesTest`, `KlausGameTests` |
| R162 | **Klaus's hunt** (`KlausRules`): who may be hunted (a doppelgänger, in the overworld, not in spectator; a villager only near a player; a player only with `klaus_hunts_players`), the roll (a roll of exactly the chance does not pass), the warning (a player only) and its reminders, the arrival that was put off, the withdrawal. One roll a night, spent only when somebody could be hunted. **He withdraws** (discarded, the index says lost, the director may send him again) at dawn (outside the full-moon night window), after 4 minutes (`GIVE_UP_AFTER`) without landing a blow on his prey, or after a minute with no prey and nobody to fight; a fallen Klaus never withdraws, and one the operator spawned (no prey) stays. | `KlausRulesTest`, `KlausGameTests` |
| R163 | **The bite**: venom (the `hybrid_venom` effect, not renewed on a victim who has it) only in the beast form and only on a vampire. | `KlausRulesTest`, `KlausGameTests` |
| R164 | **Klaus's Blood** records Klaus as the source and gives the Original's Blood effect, so a werewolf who dies with it starts the transition in his line whatever `hybrid_transition_blood` says (it never went through that check); it cures venom; with his line dead it only cures. | `KlausGameTests` |
| R165 | **Being hunted by Klaus is how a doppelgänger finds out** (the show's way): the target's `doppelganger` gene is discovered when the hunt is announced (`BloodlineAccess.reveal`), and the message says why ("...you carry the doppelgänger gene"). The howl is sent to that player alone. Nobody else is told anything. | `KlausGameTests`, `BossTextTest` |
| R166 | **Mountain ash:** a plain line holds a vampire, not Klaus and not Mikael; only a line laid by a pack Emissary holds them (both are in `#supernatural_mobs`). Every hint and the Guide say so. | `KlausGameTests` |
| R167 | **Elijah's deal** (DealRules): one deal per player ever (declining leaves no record), an oath of `elijah_oath_days` that holds through its last day, broken by a blow to Elijah or to any NPC Original but Mikael while it binds (and never after it ran out or was broken), which makes him hostile for good and removes the deal. The offer lists every NPC Original but Elijah, the same for everybody, so it never gives away the line the player is in; the answer is an id, checked against that list. A tamed animal or a minion strikes for its owner unless the Original was after that owner (`DealRules.counts`); any other mob's blow never breaks an oath. | `DealRulesTest`, `ElijahGameTests` |
| R168 | **The Elixir** (ElixirRules): saves from any death but a line death, `/kill`, the white oak end of an Original (that one counts as a line death: the Elixir is no way out of it), a death that is part of becoming something (`vampireBloodUntil` still running, Original blood, an unstable transition) and the void, once, and the Resurrection Ring goes first. | `ElixirRulesTest`, `ElixirGameTests` |
| R169 | **Rebekah** (RebekahRules): sent by a `CureMadeEvent` after `rebekah_delay_seconds`, only while she is unseen or lost; hunts the caster; withdraws after a minute with nobody to hunt; the Cure is the only event (the rite fires it, a given Cure does not); whoever made it is told the rite noticed something. | `RebekahRulesTest`, `RebekahGameTests`, `AshDaggerGameTests` |
| R170 | **Elijah's visit** (ElijahRules): once a day, to a player who knows an Original or holds an NPC Original's blood, in the overworld, on the ground, not a spectator; he fights only after an oath was broken or when hit. | `ElijahRulesTest`, `ElijahGameTests` |
| R171 | **The True Cure** is DRANK_THE_TRUE_CURE: the same change as The Cure for every species and faction, so an Original is human again and their line is untouched. | `BloodlineRulesTest`, `RebekahGameTests` |

White oak working only while the Original is open is the rule the plan named; the stake and the Ash Dagger are the two tools of the story: the dagger opens them, the stake ends them.

## Where it is in the hub

- **Admin page, section Bosses** (operators only): a boss picker and the five commands as buttons: Spawn here (X1), State (X2), Desiccate (X3), Return now (X4), Forget (X5, asks first). The settings (`boss_check_seconds`, `boss_bars_enabled`, `original_return_days`, `npc_original_stake_needs_open`, `boss_reward_on`, `mikael_enabled`, and the other eight `<boss>_enabled`, `boss_health_factor`, `boss_damage_factor`, `mikael_min_rung`, `mikael_night_chance`, `mikael_stakes_originals`, `klaus_chance_per_full_moon`, `klaus_hunts_players`, `klaus_warning_seconds`) are on the same page with the other settings (`AdminEntries`; `AdminCoverageTest` fails when a key has no row).
- **Guide**: a chapter "The bosses", last, shown when the part is on, in short paragraphs. It says plainly that if your Original dies to white oak you die too (and so does everyone in their line), gives the rung (3), and ends with a button to the Bloodline page.
- **Home, "The world now"**: a crimson diamond line when a boss is out there within 128 blocks, a steel-blue one ("An Original lies fallen nearby.") when an Original lies fallen; named only to a player who has discovered them (`WorldNowHook.BossNear`, the snapshot's `bossNear` and `bossName`; the hub protocol is now 7).
- Admin labels: "Boss: Mikael" (and the other eight), "NPC Original stake needs a desiccated target", `mikael_stakes_originals`.

## Commands

`/supernaturalbloodlines boss <boss> spawn|state|desiccate|return|reset` (permission 2). `<boss>` suggests only the bosses that have landed (Mikael, Klaus, Elijah and Rebekah). Rebekah made by `spawn` has nobody to hunt and stays until she is brought down. `spawn` refuses with the reason from `SpawnRules` (a boss that is already out there is not made twice, and Klaus is not made without Werewolves).
