# Part: bosses

Requires: **vampirism and enchanted** (the species part's lines, which the NPC Originals root) and **GeckoLib** (Enchanted already requires it, so it costs nobody anything). Needs the `species` part. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-bosses.md`. This is phase **17d**: the NPC Original base, **Mikael** (17b), **Klaus** (17c), **Elijah and Rebekah** (17d). Elijah, Kol, Rebekah, Finn and the werewolf bosses come in 17d to 17h; their switches (`<boss>_enabled`) already exist and do nothing yet.

**Mixin budget: 0 of 3** (see `docs/mixins.md`).

## What it adds

- The first **entity types** of the mod: `supernaturalbloodlines:mikael`. A plain `PathfinderMob` (not a Vampirism entity: no sun, no bite, no conversion, no Vampirism AI), never despawns, has a boss bar for players within 64 blocks. Server and client load the same classes; nothing under `parts/bosses` except `client/` names a client class (`BossServerSafetyTest` reads every import).
- **`BossIndex`** (SavedData `supernaturalbloodlines_bosses`, in the overworld's data storage): for each boss its state (`UNSEEN`, `ACTIVE`, `OPEN`, `DEAD`, `LOST`), the entity's uuid, its last dimension and place, the day it returns, whether its reward was paid, how many times it came, the day its blood may drop again, the last night rolled, and who it hunts. A bad entry is skipped, not the file.
- **`BossDirector`**: one `LevelTickEvent.Post` hook on the overworld, every `boss_check_seconds`. It marks a boss **lost** when its entity is gone from a loaded chunk for two checks in a row (an unloaded chunk says nothing), and rolls for Mikael. No vanilla spawn rules, no biome modifier. Every refusal is one table, `SpawnRules`, in this order: `disabled`, `needs_mod`, `dead`, `present`, `cooling`.
- **The NPC Original base** (`NpcOriginalBoss`): carries `ORIGINAL_ROOT` (so the line rules, `LineDeath`, `LineWarning`, the Imprisoned Original rite and Revelation treat it like a player Original) and `NPC_OPEN` (the Imprisoned Original rite's "brought down"). What a blow does is `DefeatRules` (R151), played by `NpcOriginalEvents`.
- **Mikael**: black clothes, his white oak stake in his hand. Once a night, with the chance `mikael_night_chance`, he comes for one vampire player (never an Original or an Original hybrid, R158) of at least `mikael_min_rung`, on the ground, in the overworld, outside a mountain ash ring, 24 to 40 blocks away (6 to 24 when there is no ground, or no loaded chunk, that far). The target is told ("A hunter in black is watching you", with his name only when they have discovered him, R123). He feeds on the vampires he hits (a quarter of the damage, R156). Peaceful: he never comes. If his prey is gone for a minute he withdraws and can come again. **A plain mountain ash line does not hold him**: `MountainAshLineBlock.blocks` lets a line stop the mobs of `#supernaturalbloodlines:supernatural_mobs` only when a pack Emissary laid it (R166, a GameTest shows both), so every text says "a mountain ash line laid by a pack Emissary".
- **Mikael's Stake** (`mikaels_stake`): his reward and weapon. In `#supernaturalbloodlines:white_oak`, never breaks.
- **Klaus** (`supernaturalbloodlines:klaus`, the first GeckoLib form): one entity with a synced form, `HUMAN`, `SHIFTING`, `BEAST`, so his UUID, health, boss bar and index entry never change when he shifts. The man is the player model (`textures/entity/boss/klaus.png`); the wolf is GeckoLib (`geo/entity/klaus_hybrid.geo.json`, six animations, the glow mask on the golden irises). `ShiftRenderer` (client only) picks the renderer by the form every frame: the man, then from half way through the shift the wolf. The hitbox follows the body that is drawn (0.6 x 1.95, then 0.9 x 1.5, from the swap tick). He shifts once, at half health or against a werewolf, **never while he lies desiccated**, and stays a wolf: +50% damage, +25% speed. A desiccated wolf plays and holds the death animation. **Needs Werewolves** (`modsPresent`): without it the director never sends him, and the command refuses with "a mod it needs is missing". His entity type still registers, so a world that adds Werewolves later works.
- **The hunt**: on a full moon night (`MoonRule`), with the chance `klaus_chance_per_full_moon` (0.35), once a night, for a doppelgänger: a villager that is one and has a player within 64 blocks, or (with `klaus_hunts_players`, on) a player who carries the gene. A player is warned `klaus_warning_seconds` (90) before he arrives, on the action bar and in chat, with a howl that **only that player hears** (`playNotifySound`) and a hint that names the threat (a hybrid wolf, venom bite); the action bar repeats a short line at 30 and 10 seconds. **Being hunted tells the player they carry the doppelgänger gene** (R165). A villager is never warned and he comes at once. He arrives 24 to 40 blocks away (6 to 24 when there is no ground that far). The warning is kept in the index (`arriveAt`, `reminded`), so a restart does not lose it, and it is asked again at every look: if the player dies, leaves, changes dimension, goes to spectator, the player hunt is switched off or the world is made peaceful, the arrival is dropped at once and a player who is still here is told "The wolf lost your scent."
- **Klaus's Blood** (`klaus_blood`): his reward. Counts as the blood of an Original hybrid under either `hybrid_transition_blood`, so a werewolf who drinks it and dies starts the transition in Klaus's line, and the transition death still applies. It also cures werewolf venom, like Hybrid's Blood, and says so in its tooltip. If Klaus's line is dead (`LineIndex.isDead`) it still cures but marks nobody, and says so. It is an item, not a Sire's Blood bottle. **Drinking it makes Klaus the drinker's pending sire** (`BloodDrink`, shared with Original's Blood); he becomes the sire and the line root (hidden "?" until discovered) when the hybrid transition or the vampire turning completes, and nobody is sired by the drink alone.
- **Deucalion** (`supernaturalbloodlines:deucalion`, the second GeckoLib form): one entity with the same synced form as Klaus (`HUMAN`, `SHIFTING`, `BEAST`; `ShiftRenderer` is now generic over the boss and reads the `ShiftBody` interface). The man is the player model (`deucalion_human.png`); **the glasses are the only thing on its hat layer, and `HumanoidBossRenderer` draws the layer only while `BossNpc.hatVisible()` says so**, which for him is `ShiftRules.hatVisible(form)`: false from the first shifting tick. The wolf is `deucalion_demon_wolf` (geo, animations `idle`, `walk`, `run`, `roar` while he howls, `attack_swipe` and `attack_bite` alternately, `drop_to_fours` on the swap tick, `death`) with a glow mask. He is a plain `BossNpc` (not an NPC Original: no line, no desiccation, he dies). Hitbox 0.6 by 1.95 as the man, 1.2 by 2.6 as the wolf, from the swap tick.
- **The call** (R178, `DeucalionRules`): a full moon night, once per world, for a player who is a werewolf Alpha whose **effective** stack (`PackAccess.effectiveSparks`: a True Alpha who is a werewolf counts as holding `max_stacked_sparks`, anyone else their own count) is at least `deucalion_min_sparks`, in the overworld, not a spectator; no dice. One try a night (`lastRollDay`); no Werewolves, `deucalion_enabled` off or a peaceful world keep him away. The warning gives the threshold from the config (`%s`). He appears 24 to 40 blocks from the target (6 to 24 with no ground that far), hunts them (`PreyGoal`) and withdraws at dawn, after 1200 ticks with nobody to hunt or 4800 without a hit, taking his pack with him; the hunted player is told why. Losing him or leaving with the dawn only ends that night: he comes again on a later full moon while the player still stacks enough. **Pack**: `deucalion_pack_alphas` Alphas from `NpcPackSpawner.spawnBossAlphas` (`NpcMark.bossPack`), set on his target; `DeucalionEvents` cancels their drops and experience, `WeakSpark.onDeath` returns early for them, and one that loads from disk after its night is over (or at dawn) removes itself (`DeucalionRules.packMemberStale`). The shift and the hunt are shared with Klaus: `ShiftCore` (standing still, the swap, the end, the beast's bonuses) and `HuntState` (prey, the two counts, the leaving) are used by both entities; the subclasses keep the trigger, the hitbox and the reward.
- **The reward** (R179, `DeucalionRewardRules`, `DeucalionEvents`, `DamageTally`): the damage players deal to him is counted in a `DamageTally` saved with him (a tamed animal or a Vampirism minion counts for its owner; a mob or the environment for nobody). At his death (a real one: the listener is at `LOW` and ignores a cancelled event) the index says dead and writes the day (`returnDay`), the pack goes, and the reward is paid once by `boss_reward_on` to **the player who did the most damage** (a tie goes to the last hitter; a player's own last blow only if nothing was tallied): `SparkHandler.grantFromBoss(top, deucalion_sparks)`, then `giveRoar`. The ladder is `PackRules.stealBy`: NEVER (a True Alpha: told "reward_true_alpha", still learns the Roar), PROTECTED (destroyed, "spark_destroyed"), TAKES (`SparkHandler.takeSparks`, shared with a steal: `sparksAfterSteal` with `max_stacked_sparks`, `PackEvent.KILLED_ALPHA`, "spark_gained" and, by `PackRules.capped`, "spark_capped"); somebody who is no werewolf is told "reward_no_werewolf". No branch is silent. With `deucalion_respawn` on and `deucalion_respawn_days` passed since `returnDay` the director forgets him (`UNSEEN`, reward unpaid).
- **The Demon Wolf Roar** (R180, `DemonRoarRules`, `DemonRoar`): S-WOL-11 is a granted-only skill (`data/.../skill/s-wol-11.json`, icon `VOICE`, params `range` 32 and `cooldown_ticks` 2400, which the rules read through `LadderQuery.value`) in the saved skills of a werewolf who was paid; `GrantedSkills` makes it count only while its holder is a werewolf, and the Skills page shows it as owned (not dormant) while it counts (`hub.skill_desc.s-wol-11`). `AlphaRoar.roar` calls `DemonRoar.after` once its own roar has happened: the roarer holds the skill and its cooldown is over, then every other **player** Alpha within the range, not in the roarer's pack, in the same level, is forced into a form through the action handler (beast, survival, human, the first the Werewolves mod allows; a form that is on already, an eclipse, a shock and a bound hybrid stop it). It follows the rules of hurting a player: nobody where `isPvpAllowed()` is off, nobody in spectator mode, nobody the roarer may not harm (`canHarmPlayer`, the same team). Open Parties and Claims' claims are not asked: the project has no claim check (only the party mirror). The plan said to put a flag in the roar's payload; the server already knows who holds the skill, so there is none.
- **Peter Hale** (`supernaturalbloodlines:peter_hale`, the third GeckoLib form, R181 to R183): one entity with the same synced form as Klaus and Deucalion; the man is the player model (`peter_hale_burned.png` until he has met a full moon, `peter_hale.png` after), the wolf is the quadruped Alpha (`peter_hale_alpha`, 1.4 by 1.7). He waits in a burned Hale house, feral on a full moon night, and pounces (the animation moves its root bone 30 pixels, so he stands still while it plays and is put where it ends). He drops the **Memory Claws**, and his spark goes to who did the most damage. Settings: `peter_house_enabled`, `peter_house_chance`, `peter_feral_full_moon`, `peter_respawn`, `peter_respawn_days`.
- **The Beast of Gévaudan** (R184 to R191; three bodies, three entity types: `gevaudan_host`, `beast_of_gevaudan`, `gevaudan_spirit`): the **Host** (`GevaudanHostEntity`, the man in the Valet's coat and `sebastien_valet.png`, the tricorn on his head; no boss bar) is made once on a full moon night (chance `beast_host_chance`, 0.25) near an Alpha, a True Alpha or a Hunter of the Five, with a notice and a hint that never name it; he is invulnerable to everything but the void and `/kill`, and his death fails the night (a Host is made again once, at most twice in a world). On a later full moon night (or the Rite of the Frequency, or the Admin Change button) he becomes the **Beast** (`BeastEntity`, GeckoLib `beast_of_gevaudan`, 2.4 by 3.0, 420 health, ten animations; no ShiftRenderer, the Host is a separate body): it Roars after a 40-tick rear-up (Weakness II and Darkness for `beast_roar_seconds`, range `beast_roar_range`, every `beast_roar_cooldown_seconds`; Alphas and True Alphas are immune, a hostile mob of no pack runs), heals 2 health a second unless any wolfsbane strain (or Weakness with Nausea) is on it, takes 0.6 of what shots do, takes a heart at most from a wolfsbane pulse, and is stunned for a second at most by a Hearing Device. It cannot die of blows: at a quarter of its health, or inside a closed ring of mountain ash (`beast_respects_ash`), it **collapses**: the Host stands up where it fell (the index's now) and the **Spirit** (flying smoke, no model) runs for `beast_spirit_seconds`. Only the **Valet Pike** hurts the Spirit and one blow ends the Beast for good. If it escapes it comes back **once** at half health on a later full moon night (`beast_returns_once`); the second escape is for good and ends the encounter with no reward. One entity of the boss is the index's at a time: the next body is written down before the last one goes. The reward is the **Mantle of the Beast** (R191), once, to the player who did the most damage to the Beast over the whole encounter (the tally is in the index, so it outlives the body; a tie goes to the last hitter; a pet counts for its owner), else to whoever struck with the pike, else where the Spirit fell; no spark, no pike. No Werewolves, no Beast.
- **Elijah** (`supernaturalbloodlines:elijah`, calm): once a day, with the chance `elijah_chance_per_day` (0.05), by day or by night (and in a peaceful world), he comes 8 to 20 blocks from a player who has discovered an Original or carries the blood of an NPC Original (R170). The visit names him only to someone who has discovered him. He fights only the one who hit him, until an oath is broken: then he is hostile for good. **Right-click opens the deal** (R167): a choice dialog in the hub's own look (`ElijahDealScreen`: the player's hub theme, the kit's `ChoiceLayout` and `ChoiceModel`, not a hub page). It says what you pick, the oath, for how many in-game days, and what breaking it costs; its buttons scroll when they do not fit (GUI scale 4 at 720p) and it is read out by the narrator. It lists every NPC Original but Elijah, the same for everybody: leaving one out would tell a player which line they are in. Picking the Original whose line you are in is allowed (the vial has a name on it and nothing more). The answer is that Original's id; the server checks it against the list it would offer and says why on the action bar when it refuses. He stands still, facing the player, while an offer is open (60 seconds). The vial is a Sire's Blood bottle of that Original, and the player swears an oath for `elijah_oath_days` (7) in-game days: not to harm or desiccate any NPC Original but Mikael, and not to strike Elijah. A player is offered one deal, ever; declining leaves no record. A blow to Elijah, Klaus or Rebekah while the oath binds breaks it, writes a broken record, makes Elijah hostile for good (`BossIndex.Entry.hostile`) and removes the deal for everybody. A tamed animal or a Vampirism minion strikes for its owner (the oath is about what you set on them too), unless the Original was already after that owner or had been hurt by them: then it is defending them and nothing counts; any other mob's blow never breaks anything.
- **The Elixir** (`the_elixir`): Elijah's reward. Carried in the inventory, it brings its holder back from the next death that is not a line death, not `/kill`, not the white oak end of an Original, not a death that is part of becoming something (vampire blood in the veins, an unstable hybrid transition, Original blood) and not the void (the tooltip and the Guide say so), once, at `elixir_heal_fraction` (0.5) of their health (R168). A death the Resurrection Ring would save is left to the ring (`RingProbe`).
- **Rebekah** (`supernaturalbloodlines:rebekah`, slim skin): the Rite of the Cure fires `CureMadeEvent` (U2) when it makes a Cure; `rebekah_delay_seconds` (60) later she arrives 12 to 48 blocks from the circle and hunts whoever cast it (R169). She does not answer a Cure made while she lies desiccated, is dead, is out there already or is switched off, a `/give` or a dropped Cure is no event, and she never comes into a peaceful world. With nobody left to hunt for a minute she withdraws and may come again. The one who made the Cure is told at once "The rite is done. Something old has noticed."; the one she hunts is told "Rebekah is here" only if they have discovered her, else "Someone is here". A mountain ash line laid by a pack Emissary holds her and Elijah, a plain one does not (GameTest, like Klaus and Mikael: R166).
- **Kol** (`supernaturalbloodlines:kol`): by night, once a night, he comes 16 to 32 blocks from a Hunter of the Five whose mark has at least `kol_min_mark` (12) kills, in the overworld, on the ground, not a spectator (R173), and hunts them (`PreyGoal`). He is fast and has one trick, a leap at his target from 3 to 9 blocks away with an 80-tick pause (`BossLeapGoal`). With nobody to hunt and nobody to fight for a minute he withdraws and may come again the next night. The hunted player is told "Something fast is coming for you in the dark. It knows how many you have hunted." (his name only once discovered) and "A mountain ash line laid by a pack Emissary will hold him." (tested, like the others).
- **Kol's Grimoire** (`kol_grimoire`): Kol's reward. Right-click reads it, once per copy: `kol_grimoire_sp` (5) skill points into the one shared pool (`SupernaturalState.sp`, open question 12), and the copy is marked read. The **Rite of Cloaking** (`rite_of_cloaking`, factory `cloaking`, medium circle, 4000 power, 2 phantom membrane + 1 ender pearl + the grimoire, conditioned on the bosses part) needs a witch, siphoner, heretic or tribrid performer and a grimoire that has been read; it sets the performer's `CLOAK_UNTIL` for `kol_cloak_minutes` (30) and gives the grimoire back (R172, R174). When the cloak runs out the player is told once, "Your cloak fades." (`CloakEvents`); dying ends it silently. A cloaked entity is left out of the Vampire Compass (`VampireCompassItem.nearestVampire`) and of a Hunter of the Five's sense (`HunterInstinct.senses`): two one-line checks, no mixin. A new cloak never shortens a running one; death ends it (the attachment is not copied on death).
- **Finn** (`supernaturalbloodlines:finn`): under a lunar eclipse, once a night, with the chance `finn_chance_per_eclipse` (0.5), he appears 32 to 64 blocks from a witch-type or vampire player in the overworld (R175). He stands in a ring of particles and channels a ritual for `finn_ritual_seconds` (120) that **hurts nobody**: left alone he finishes it, everyone within 128 blocks is told "Something was finished under the eclipse. Nobody was hurt.", and he withdraws until a later eclipse; the player he came for is told when he arrives, and then "Left alone, the ritual hurts no one. Hurt him badly and he will fight." (the next roll is 7 days away). Hurt for more than a fifth of his health, or pulled more than 24 blocks from the ring (R176), he breaks the ritual off, the people near are told, and he fights like anyone else (he targets nobody during the ritual). If the eclipse ends before he is done he leaves with nothing done and no rest. A mountain ash line laid by a pack Emissary holds him (tested).
- **Esther's Grimoire** (`esther_grimoire`): Finn's reward, and the key of the **Linking Spell** (`rite_of_linking`, factory `linking_spell`, medium circle, 5000 power, 2 lead + 2 amethyst shards + the grimoire, `faction: witch`). A **witch-type performer** (witch, siphoner, heretic or tribrid; a plain player is told so and gets everything back). Exactly two living creatures stand in the circle: within **5.5 blocks** of its centre (the chalk is 11 wide) and with their **feet** at most 2 above or below the altar; the performer counts if they stand in it, so a witch can link herself to a creature. Neither may be an **NPC Original** (visibly a boss, so refusing it reveals nothing) or any boss (`BossNpc`, `#c:bosses`, anything that cannot change dimensions), or linked already. **A player Original may be linked** like anyone who agrees (Esther linked her children). Every other **player** agrees by **sneaking inside the circle with a lead in the main hand** (a deliberate act), and only where PvP is on. Links that ran out are cleared at the start of the rite. For `link_seconds` (60) `link_damage_share` (0.5) of the damage one takes is dealt to the other, once, **in the performer's name** (`indirectMagic(null, performer)`: teams, claims, PvP logs and kill credit see them; to the performer herself it is plain magic damage). The partner is marked as sharing while it takes the hit, so the hit it makes cannot be shared again, whoever is the attacker. A player partner's own hit cooldown is never skipped. It ends with a death, a dimension change or its time; a linked player is told when it starts and when it ends. **Every refusal is one message, "The link will not hold.", and costs the same**: the lead, the amethyst and the altar's power are spent (the grimoire always comes back), so a refusal never tells a player who is a boss or what was wrong; the wrong-count case is in the Guide, not in the message. This is deliberate (R177). What an unloaded partner owes is stored only for creatures, never for players, and a creature that loads takes at most half its maximum health of it. The links (and who made them) are saved in `LinkIndex`.
- **The True Cure** (`the_true_cure`): Rebekah's reward. One drink clears every layer (`DRANK_THE_TRUE_CURE`, the same change as The Cure, R171); an Original drinks it willingly with no rite and no desiccation, and the ones they sired keep their line (a cured Original is not a line death).

| R161 | **The shift** (`ShiftRules`): only the human form shifts, once, at half health or less (alive) or when the one he fights is a werewolf, and never while he is desiccated; it takes 40 ticks and he stands still meanwhile. **On tick 20 (`SWAP_TICK`) the body is swapped**: the wolf model and hitbox from that tick, a burst of smoke, a howl sound and a short shake.
